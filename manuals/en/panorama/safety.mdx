## Safety Instructions

Always use this product in a safe and responsible manner. Read the following guidelines carefully to avoid risks such as electric shock or fire.

### Before use

- Remove the protective film from the screen surface.
- Only use an AC/DC adapter as power supply. Place it in a well-ventilated area, away from heat sources.
- Make sure the power outlet is properly grounded and suitable for the correct amperage.
- The required DC input is between 5V and 20V (with a tolerance of ±2V).
- Avoid humid spaces and dust build-up to prevent damage to the electronics.
- Protect the screen from impacts and external pressure.
- Do not use sharp objects on or around the screen.
- Suitable for both home and professional use.
- Recommended ambient temperature: between -20°C and 60°C.
- Turn off the screen when you are not using it.

### Folding caution

Watch your fingers when folding the screens in or out to avoid pinching.
