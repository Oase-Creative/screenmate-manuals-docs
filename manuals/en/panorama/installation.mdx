## Physical Setup

Place the Screenmate on your desk before unfolding the screens.

1. Position the Screenmate on your desk as shown in the diagram.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Panorama%20-%20Handleiding%20images/installation-step-1.png" alt="Place the folded Screenmate on your desk" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

2. Fold the locking legs inwards.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Panorama%20-%20Handleiding%20images/installation-step-2.png" alt="Fold the locking legs inwards" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

3. Grip the screens and pull them upwards until the legs sit in the front lock.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Panorama%20-%20Handleiding%20images/installation-step-3.png" alt="Pull the screens upwards until the legs sit in the front lock" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

4. Rotate the screens upwards, then fold them open to your desired viewing angle.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Panorama%20-%20Handleiding%20images/installation-step-4.png" alt="Fold the screens open to your desired viewing angle" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

<Note>
**Caution:** Watch your fingers when folding the screens to avoid pinching.
</Note>

To fold the Panorama back up, perform the steps above in reverse order.

## Install the Display Driver

Before connecting the monitor to your PC or laptop for the first time, you must install the correct display driver. The Screenmate Panorama needs this driver to drive multiple screens through a single cable.

Choose the software that matches your laptop's operating system, download the file, unzip the archive, and install the application by following the on-screen steps.

Download the driver from the Silicon Motion download page:  
[https://www.siliconmotion.com/downloads/index.html](https://www.siliconmotion.com/downloads/index.html)

## Connection Options

### Option 1 — USB-C (single cable)

For laptops with a USB-C port that supports video output (DisplayPort Alt Mode).

1. Connect the 65 W power adapter to the monitor's USB-C charging port using the supplied USB-C to USB-C cable.
2. Connect the monitor to your laptop's USB-C port with a second USB-C to USB-C cable.

<Info>
Use the long white cable for power, and the short black cable to connect the Panorama to your laptop.
</Info>

<Note>
A laptop's USB-C port does not supply enough power on its own to run the Panorama at full brightness. Always connect the 65 W adapter for stable operation.
</Note>

<Note>
The Panorama can charge your laptop over USB-C (reverse charging). Connecting a separate charger to your laptop while the Panorama is also providing power may cause interference.
</Note>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Panorama%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-1-usb-c.png" alt="USB-C connection diagram: laptop, Screenmate Panorama and power adapter" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### Option 2 — USB-A + HDMI

For laptops where the USB-C port does not support video output. An extra HDMI cable is required to drive all three screens.

1. Connect the 65 W power adapter to the monitor via the USB-C to USB-C charging cable.
2. Connect the monitor to a USB-A port on your laptop using a USB-A to USB-C cable.
3. Finally, connect your laptop's HDMI port to the monitor using the Mini HDMI to HDMI cable. On the monitor, connect the HDMI cable to the HDMI port next to the white power cable.
