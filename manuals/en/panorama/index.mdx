## What is the Screenmate Panorama?

The Screenmate Panorama is a triple-screen portable monitor that gives you three 15.6" Full HD displays from a single device. It connects to your laptop with a single USB-C cable (or USB-A + HDMI as a fallback) and is designed to massively expand your workspace on the go. A required display driver allows three independent screens to be driven through one cable.

## Package Contents

- **Screenmate Panorama**
- **1x USB-C to USB-C cable (1.2 m)**
- **1x USB-C to USB-C cable (0.5 m)**
- **1x Mini-HDMI to HDMI cable**
- **65 Watt USB-C power adapter**
