## Installation Instructions

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/installation-steps.png" alt="Screenmate Flip installation steps" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

1. Carefully remove the Screenmate from the packaging.
2. Fold out the right screen in the direction shown in image 2.
3. Fold out the left screen as shown in image 3.
4. Rotate the left screen 180° in the indicated direction.
5. Place the Screenmate on a flat surface and fold out the adjustable stand for extra support (see image 6).
6. Connect the Screenmate to your laptop with the correct cables.

## Connection Options

The Screenmate Flip supports two connection scenarios. Pick the one that matches the ports on your laptop.

### 1. Two USB-C Cables

If your laptop has two USB-C ports, use the two included USB-C cables to connect your laptop to the Screenmate.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-1-2x-usb-c.png" alt="Connection with two USB-C cables" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 2. One USB-C Cable, One HDMI Cable and One USB-A Cable

If your laptop has one USB-C port, an HDMI port and a USB-A port, connect the Screenmate as follows:

Use the included USB-C cable to connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop. Connect the other side using the HDMI cable and the USB-C to USB-A cable.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-2-usb-c-hdmi-usb-a.png" alt="Connection with USB-C, HDMI and USB-A cables" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

<Note>
**Please note:** the HDMI port does not supply power, so the Screenmate must be connected to a power source of 5V/2A or higher. This can easily be done via your laptop's USB-A port.
</Note>

## Storing the Screenmate

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/storage-steps.png" alt="Screenmate Flip storage steps" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

1. Disconnect the Screenmate from your laptop.
2. Rotate the left screen 180° back in the direction shown in image 1.
3. Fold both screens in as shown in image 2.
4. Place the Screenmate in the protective sleeve to prevent damage and store it in a safe place.
