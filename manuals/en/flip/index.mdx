## What is the Screenmate Flip?

The Screenmate Flip is a foldable multi-screen monitor that clips around your laptop to give you two extra displays for more overview and focus. Both side screens fold flat against the central housing, making it easy to take your multi-monitor workspace wherever you go.

The Flip is available in two sizes:

- **Flip 14"** — two extra 14" WUXGA (1920×1200) screens with a 16:10 aspect ratio.
- **Flip 15.6"** — two extra 15.6" Full HD (1920×1080) screens with a 16:9 aspect ratio.

Select your size on the specifications table below to see the exact details for your model.

<Note>
**Important Information:**

Take good care of your Screenmate Flip and do not press on the screens to avoid damage. Do not rotate the screens beyond the maximum angles shown below.

- **Left** screen: 0° – 245° (180° tiltable up and down)
- **Right** screen: 0° – 205° (tilts up and down, just like the left screen)

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/warning-rotation.png" alt="Screen rotation angle limits" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200 mt-2" />

The Screenmate consumes a small amount of power in standby mode. For energy-efficient operation, we recommend disconnecting the power cable when the monitor is not in use.
</Note>
