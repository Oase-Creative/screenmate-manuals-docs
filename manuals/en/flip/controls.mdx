## Ports and Buttons

This section gives an overview of all physical ports and control buttons on the Screenmate Flip.

### Side Ports

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/ports-side.png" alt="Screenmate Flip side ports" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **USB-C Port:** power supply and video transmission.
- **USB-C Port:** power supply and video transmission.
- **Mini HDMI:** video transmission only (no power).

### Control Buttons

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/buttons-bottom.png" alt="Screenmate Flip control buttons" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **M** — OSD menu button.
- **+** — Increase brightness.
- **−** — Decrease brightness.
