## Ports and Buttons

This section provides an overview of all physical ports and control buttons on the Screenmate Expand.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/ports-buttons.png" alt="Screenmate Expand ports and buttons overview" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### Buttons

- **− Decrease brightness:** Lowers screen brightness.
- **+ Increase brightness:** Raises screen brightness.
- **≡ Menu button:** Opens the on-screen menu (OSD). See [On-Screen Menu](/manuals/en/expand/osd) for the full settings reference.

### Ports

- **Mini HDMI:** Video transfer (no power).
- **USB-C Port:** Power supply and video transfer.
- **USB-C Port:** Power supply and video transfer.
