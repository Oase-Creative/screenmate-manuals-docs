## What is the Screenmate Lite 144 Hz?

The Screenmate Lite 144 Hz is a lightweight 15.6" Full HD portable monitor with a fast 144 Hz refresh rate, built to give gamers and power users a smooth second screen for laptops, phones, tablets, and game consoles. It connects via USB-C or HDMI and weighs just 609 grams.
