## Ports and Buttons

This section provides an overview of all physical ports and control buttons on the Screenmate OneCable monitor.

### USB-C Port (Data/Video/Power)

Connect your laptop cable here. This port receives data and video from your laptop and can also receive power.

This is the main port for connecting your laptop via USB-C.

### USB-C Port (Power Only / Power Delivery)

If extra power is needed, or when you want to use the reverse charging function, you can connect a USB-C cable here.

<img

  alt="USB-C Power Delivery icon"

This port only receives power and is used for:
- Additional power supply when your laptop doesn't deliver enough power
- Reverse charging: charge your laptop via the Screenmate
- Automatic screen brightness optimization when using Power Delivery

<Note>
**Note:** Make sure you use the correct USB-C port. The main port is for data/video, the Power port is for power only.
</Note>

### Brightness Buttons

The Screenmate OneCable has separate brightness buttons for each screen:

- **Brightness - (Left screen)** / **Brightness - (Right screen)**: Decrease the brightness of the left or right screen
- **Brightness + (Left screen)** / **Brightness + (Right screen)**: Increase the brightness of the left or right screen

You can adjust the brightness of both screens independently.
