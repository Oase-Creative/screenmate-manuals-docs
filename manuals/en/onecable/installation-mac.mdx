### Installation Steps

1. Download the driver via the button above.
2. Go to the 'macOS' folder.
3. Install the driver following the installer instructions.
4. Go to System Preferences > Security & Privacy > Screen Recording.
5. Click 'Unlock' (you'll need your Apple ID and password) and check 'UsbDisplay'.
6. After successful installation, restart your Mac.
7. Connect the Screenmate – it's now ready for use.

## If the Driver Doesn't Work

Follow these steps on your MacBook:

1. Go to "System Settings" > "Privacy & Security" > "Screen & System Audio Recording" ('Scherm- en systeemaudio-opname')
2. Click the "+" symbol to add the driver. Is 'UsbDisplay' already in the list? Then proceed directly to step 5.
3. Click on Applications > RacerUSB > usbDisplay and choose Open.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to install the driver.
5. After successful installation, restart your laptop.
6. Connect the Screenmate to your laptop, it's now ready for use.
