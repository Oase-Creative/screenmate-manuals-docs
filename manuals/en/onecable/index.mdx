## What is the Screenmate OneCable?

The Screenmate OneCable is a portable monitor designed to boost your productivity by adding two extra screens to your laptop with just one cable connection.

| **Size** | 16" |

| **Dimensions (folded)** | 39 x 24 x 2.5 cm |
| **Color** | Grey |

<Note>
**Important Information:**

Take good care of your Screenmate OneCable and do not press on the screens to prevent damage. Do not rotate the screens beyond the maximum angle: Left screen (0° - 360°), Right screen (0° - 330°).

The Screenmate consumes a small amount of power in standby mode. For energy-efficient operation, we recommend disconnecting the power cable when the monitor is not in use.
</Note>
