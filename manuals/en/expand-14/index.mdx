## What is the Screenmate Expand 14"?

The Screenmate Expand 14" is a dual-screen extension that clips onto the back of your laptop and adds two 14" Full HD displays — one on each side — for a true triple-screen workspace on the go.

## Package Contents

- **Screenmate Expand** — the dual-screen unit
- **2x USB-C to USB-C cables** — video and power
- **2x Mini-HDMI to HDMI cables** — video only
- **2x USB-C to USB-A cables** — power only
- **Protective case**
- **Cable organizer**
- **6x protective clips**

<Note>
**Important Information:**

Take good care of your Screenmate Expand and do not press on the screens to prevent damage. Do not rotate the screens beyond the maximum angles indicated below.

- **Left screen:** 0° – 235°
- **Right screen:** 0° – 180°
