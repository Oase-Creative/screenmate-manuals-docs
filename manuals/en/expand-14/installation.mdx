## Choose Your Cables

To work out which cables you need, first check which ports your laptop has. The Screenmate Expand can be connected in one of the following ways:

- **2x USB-C**
- **1x USB-C & 1x USB-A & 1x HDMI**
- **2x USB-A & 2x HDMI**

## Protective Clip

If your laptop screen is less than 6 mm thick, use the protective clip so the Screenmate sits more securely on your display.

## Setup Steps

1. Unpack the Screenmate.
2. Fold the two screens open in the direction shown in illustration 2.
3. Place the bracket against the back of your laptop screen and make sure it sits firmly so the screen is clamped properly.
4. Lift the kickstand and extend it fully while holding the button pressed down. (The kickstand is built into the back of the Screenmate and provides extra support. Press the button again to retract it.)
5. Connect the appropriate cables: one end into your laptop, the other into the Screenmate.
6. See **Ports and Buttons** for an overview of all available connections.

## Connection Options

The Screenmate Expand 14" supports three connection scenarios. Pick the one that matches your laptop's available ports.

### 1. 2x USB-C cables

If your laptop has two USB-C ports, use the two included USB-C cables to connect your laptop to the Screenmate.

<Note>
**Important:** make sure you use the correct USB-C port on your Screenmate.
</Note>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-1-usb-c.png" alt="Connection with two USB-C cables" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 2. 1x USB-C cable, 1x HDMI cable and 1x USB-A cable

If your laptop has one USB-C port, an HDMI port and a USB-A port, connect the Screenmate as follows:

Use the included USB-C cable to connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop. Connect the other side with the HDMI cable and the USB-C to USB-A cable.

<Note>
**Important:** the HDMI port does not deliver power, so the Screenmate must be connected to a 5V/2A or higher power source. This can easily be done via your laptop's USB-A port.
</Note>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-2-usb-c-hdmi-usb-a.png" alt="Connection with one USB-C, one HDMI and one USB-A cable" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 3. 2x USB-A and 2x HDMI

If your laptop does not have enough HDMI ports, use a suitable HDMI hub or adapter. This connection requires four cables plus an extra hub or adapter.

**Steps:**

1. Connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop with an HDMI cable and a USB-C to USB-A cable.
2. Connect the HDMI hub or adapter to your laptop. Then connect the other side of the Screenmate with a second HDMI cable and an additional USB-C to USB-A cable.

<Note>
**Important:** the HDMI port does not deliver power, so the Screenmate must be connected to a 5V/2A or higher power source. This can easily be done via your laptop's USB-A port.
</Note>
