## Introduction to the OSD

1. Press the **M (Menu)** button to open the OSD menu.
2. Use the **+/−** buttons to select your desired setting.
3. Press the **M (Menu)** button to confirm your choice.
4. Press and hold the **M (Menu)** button to return to the previous page.

## 1. Backlight

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-backlight.png" alt="OSD Backlight menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Brightness:** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Contrast:** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **ECO mode:** Preset display profiles for different use cases (Standard, Game, Movie, Text, FPS, RTS, Energy Saving).
- **DCR (Dynamic Contrast Ratio):** Set to ON or OFF to enable or disable the dynamic contrast function.
- **Sharpness:** Adjustable from 0 to 4.

## 2. Image

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-image.png" alt="OSD Image menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Aspect:** Switch the aspect ratio between 4:3 and WIDE.

## 3. Color

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-color.png" alt="OSD Color menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Color Temp.:** Set the RGB values manually or choose a preset (Warm, Cool, User, Standard).
- **Red:** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Green:** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Blue:** Adjustable from 0 to 100.

## 4. Set

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-set.png" alt="OSD Set menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Language:** Choose the OSD language. Available languages: English, French, German, Simplified Chinese, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Polish, Dutch, Japanese, Korean.
- **OSD Timer:** Set the time after which the settings menu closes automatically (10–60 seconds).
- **Transparency:** Adjust the transparency of the settings menu.

## 5. Reset

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-reset.png" alt="OSD Reset menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Reset:** Choose RESET to restore all settings to factory defaults.
- **HDR Mode:** Enable HDR (High Dynamic Range) if your device is compatible. Available modes: Off, Auto, 2084.

## 6. Other

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-other.png" alt="OSD Other menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Source:** Choose from two signal sources: Type-C1 / Type-C2 and HDMI.
- **Low Blue Light:** Reduces the amount of blue light on the screen to ease eye strain.
