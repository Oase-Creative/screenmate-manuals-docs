## Display Configuration

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Windows">
    **Want to extend your workspace?**
    Open **Display Settings** and choose **"Extend desktop to this display"**.

    Use the **Identify** button to see which screen has which number. You can then drag the screens into the correct order.

    <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/display-windows.png" alt="Windows display settings" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

    <video controls className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-700 my-4">

      Your browser does not support the video tag.
    </video>

    **Is a screen upside down?**
    Select the screen, go to **Display orientation** and choose **"Flipped"** to correct it.

    **Want more on-screen space?**
    Click **Scale** and set it to **150%** for larger text and elements.

    <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/display-windows-scaling.png" alt="Windows scaling, resolution and orientation settings" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="macOS">
    1. Open **System Settings**.
    2. Go to **Displays**.
    3. Click **Arrange** as shown in image 1.
    4. Drag the displays into the desired order as shown in image 2.

    <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/display-macos-displays.png" alt="macOS Displays panel with the Arrange button" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

    <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/display-macos.png" alt="macOS display arrangement" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

    <video controls className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-700 my-4">
      <source src="https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/oase-documentation.firebasestorage.app/o/screenmate%2FoneCable%2Fmac-display-settings-guide-walkthrough-video.mp4?alt=media&token=ee54a819-6042-49bd-bb26-5eabb5073384" type="video/mp4" />
      Your browser does not support the video tag.
    </video>

    **Is a screen upside down?**
    Select the screen, go to **Rotation** and choose **"Standard"** to correct it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Sound Settings

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Windows">
    - Click the speaker icon in the bottom-right of the taskbar.

    <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/sound-windows-taskbar.png" alt="Windows taskbar volume icon" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

    - On the right side of the volume bar, choose the output device you want.

    <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/sound-windows-flyout.png" alt="Windows volume flyout" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

    - You can adjust the volume per application individually.
    - At the very bottom you'll find a shortcut to the full sound settings.

    <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/sound-windows.png" alt="Windows sound output settings" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

    Select the **Screenmate** output device — it is listed as **S6-L (HD Audio Driver for Display Audio)** (the other screen appears as **S6-R**). In the screenshot, the highlighted **Speaker (Realtek(R) Audio)** is your laptop's own built-in speaker, not the Screenmate — choose **S6-L** instead.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="macOS">
    1. Open the Apple menu and go to **System Settings**.
    2. Click **Sound** in the sidebar.
    3. Go to the **Output** tab.
    4. Select the **Screenmate** output device — it is listed as **S6-R (DisplayPort)** (the other screen appears as **S6-L**). In the screenshot the *MacBook speakers* are highlighted, but you must choose the **S6-R / Screenmate** entry to hear sound through your Screenmate.

    <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/sound-macos.png" alt="macOS sound settings" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
