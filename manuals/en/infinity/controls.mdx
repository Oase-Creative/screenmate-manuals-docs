## Ports and Buttons

This section gives an overview of all physical ports and control buttons on the Screenmate Infinity. The layout is mirrored on both the left and right screen.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/ports-buttons-diagram.png" alt="Screenmate Infinity ports and buttons overview" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### Speaker

Each screen has a built-in speaker on its outer edge.

### Multifunctional Button

A rotatable button on the back of each screen that controls power, the on-screen menu, brightness, and volume. See the [On-Screen Menu](#on-screen-menu-osd) section below for the full gesture reference.

### USB-C

For power and video over a USB-C to USB-C cable. The port on each screen is marked with a **blue sticker** on the back.

### USB-C (for HDMI > USB-C)

A second USB-C port intended for the HDMI to USB-C cable. This port is marked with a **red sticker** on the back.

## On-Screen Menu (OSD)

The Screenmate Infinity is controlled via the rotatable multifunctional button on the back of each screen. The same gestures work for both screens.

### Menu / Select / Confirm

- **Short press:** open the menu or confirm a selection.
- **Press and hold (2 seconds):** go back.
- **Press and hold (3 seconds):** turn the screen off.

### Brightness and volume

- **Press right ("Plus"):** increase the backlight (brightness).
- **Press left ("Min"):** decrease the volume.
