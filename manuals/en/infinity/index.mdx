## What is the Screenmate Infinity?

The Screenmate Infinity is a foldable dual 15.6" Full HD portable monitor that clips behind your laptop to give you two extra screens at once. Each screen has built-in speakers and connects via USB-C or HDMI for instant triple-screen productivity on the go.
