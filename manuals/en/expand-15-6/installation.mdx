First check which ports your laptop has. Use the icons below as a reference.

The Screenmate can be connected in the following ways:

- 2x USB-C
- 1x USB-C & 1x USB-A & 1x HDMI
- 2x USB-A & 2x HDMI

## Protective Cap

If your laptop screen is less than 6 mm thick, use the protective cap so the Screenmate fits snugly on your display.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/protective-cap.png" alt="Protective cap attached to the Screenmate brackets" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

## Installation Steps

1. Unpack the Screenmate.
2. Unfold the two screens in the direction shown in image 2.
3. Place the bracket against the back of your laptop screen and make sure it grips firmly so your screen is clamped tightly.
4. Lift the stand and pull it out while holding down the button. The stand is built into the back of the Screenmate and provides extra support. Press the button again to slide the stand back in.
5. Connect the right cables: plug one end into your laptop and the other into the Screenmate. See [Ports and Buttons](/manuals/en/expand-15-6/controls) for an overview of every available connection.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/installation-steps.png" alt="Installation steps 1 through 6" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

## Connection Options

The Screenmate Expand 15.6" supports three connection scenarios. Pick the one that matches the ports on your laptop.

### 1. Two USB-C Cables

If your laptop has two USB-C ports, use the two included USB-C cables to connect your laptop to the Screenmate. Each cable carries both video and power.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-1-usb-c.png" alt="Two USB-C cables connecting laptop to Screenmate Expand" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 2. 1x USB-C, 1x HDMI and 1x USB-A

If your laptop has one USB-C port, one HDMI port and one USB-A port, you can connect the Screenmate as follows:

Use the included USB-C cable to connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop. Connect the other side with the HDMI cable and the USB-C to USB-A cable.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-2-mixed.png" alt="USB-C, HDMI and USB-A connection to Screenmate Expand" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

<Note>
**Note:** The HDMI port does not deliver power, so the Screenmate must be connected to a power source of 5V/2A or higher. The USB-A port on your laptop works well for this.
</Note>

### 3. 2x USB-A and 2x HDMI

If your laptop doesn't have enough HDMI ports, use a compatible HDMI hub or adapter. This setup needs four cables plus an extra hub or adapter.

**Steps:**

1. Connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop using an HDMI cable and a USB-C to USB-A cable.
2. Plug the HDMI hub or adapter into your laptop, then connect the other side of the Screenmate using a second HDMI cable and another USB-C to USB-A cable.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-3-hdmi.png" alt="Two HDMI and two USB-A connections to Screenmate Expand via a hub" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

<Note>
**Note:** The HDMI port does not deliver power. Connect the Screenmate to a power source — for example via the USB-A port on your laptop.
</Note>
