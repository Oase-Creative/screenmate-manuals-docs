## What is the Screenmate Expand 15.6"?

The Screenmate Expand 15.6" is a triple-screen portable monitor that clips onto your laptop and unfolds two 15.6" Full HD displays — one on each side — turning your laptop into a three-screen workstation.

## Package Contents

- **Screenmate Expand 15.6"** — the dual-screen unit
- **2x USB-C to USB-C cables** — video and power
- **2x Mini-HDMI to HDMI cables** — video only
- **2x USB-C to USB-A cables** — power only
- **Protective case**
- **Cable organizer**
- **6x protective clips**

<Note>
**Important Information:**

Take good care of your Screenmate Expand and do not press on the screens to prevent damage. Do not rotate the screens beyond the maximum angle shown below.

- **Left screen:** 0° – 235°
- **Right screen:** 0° – 180°

The Screenmate consumes a small amount of power in standby mode. For energy-efficient operation, we recommend disconnecting the power cable when the monitor is not in use.
