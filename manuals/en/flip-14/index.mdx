## What is the Screenmate Flip 14"?

The Screenmate Flip 14" is a foldable triple-screen monitor that clips around your laptop to give you two extra 14" WUXGA (1920×1200) displays. Both side screens fold flat against the central housing, making it easy to take your multi-monitor workspace wherever you go.

## What's in the Box?

- **Screenmate Flip 14"** — the foldable triple-screen unit
- **1× USB-C with USB-A Adapter (90 cm)** — USB-C port: video and power / USB-A port: power only
- **2× USB-C to USB-C Cables (60 cm)** — video and power
- **1× Mini HDMI to HDMI Cable (90 cm)** — video only
- **Protective Sleeve**

<Note>
**Important Information:**

Take good care of your Screenmate Flip and do not press on the screens to avoid damage. Do not rotate the screens beyond the maximum angles shown below.

- **Left** screen: 0° – 245° (180° tiltable up and down)
- **Right** screen: 0° – 205°

The Screenmate consumes a small amount of power in standby mode. For energy-efficient operation, we recommend disconnecting the power cable when the monitor is not in use.
</Note>
