## Ports and Buttons

This section provides an overview of all physical ports and control buttons on the Screenmate Flip 14".

### Side Ports

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/ports-side.png" alt="Screenmate Flip 14 inch side ports" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **USB-C Port:** Power supply and video transfer.
- **USB-C Port:** Power supply and video transfer.
- **Mini HDMI:** Video transfer only (no power).

### Control Buttons

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/ports-buttons.png" alt="Screenmate Flip 14 inch control buttons" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **M:** OSD menu button.
- **+:** Increase brightness.
- **−:** Decrease brightness.
