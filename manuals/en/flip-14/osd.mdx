## Using the OSD Menu

1. Press the **M (Menu)** button to open the OSD menu.
2. Use the **+ / −** buttons to select your preferred settings.
3. Press the **M (Menu)** button to confirm your choice.
4. Press and hold the **M (Menu)** button to return to the previous page.

## 1. Backlight

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-backlight.png" alt="OSD Backlight menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Brightness (0–100):** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Contrast (0–100):** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **ECO mode:** Preset picture modes for different use cases — Standard, Game, Movie, Text, FPS, RTS, Energy Saving.
- **DCR (Dynamic Contrast Ratio):** Choose ON or OFF to enable or disable the dynamic contrast function.
- **Sharpness (0–4):** Adjustable from 0 to 4.

## 2. Image

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-image.png" alt="OSD Image menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Aspect Ratio:** Switch the aspect ratio between 4:3 and WIDE.

## 3. Color

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-color.png" alt="OSD Color menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Color Temperature:** Manually set the RGB values or choose a preset mode (Warm, Cool, User, Standard).
- **Red (0–100):** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Green (0–100):** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Blue (0–100):** Adjustable from 0 to 100.

## 4. Settings

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-set.png" alt="OSD Settings menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Language:** Choose the OSD language. Available languages: English, French, German, Simplified Chinese, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Polish, Dutch, Japanese, Korean.
- **OSD Timer (10–60 seconds):** Set how long the settings menu stays visible before closing automatically.
- **Transparency:** Adjust the transparency of the settings menu.

## 5. Reset

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-reset.png" alt="OSD Reset menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Reset:** Choose RESET to restore all settings to factory defaults.
- **HDR Mode:** Enable HDR (High Dynamic Range) if the device is compatible. Available modes: Off, Auto, 2084.

## 6. Other

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-other.png" alt="OSD Other menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Source Selection:** Choose between two signal sources — Type-C1 / Type-C2 and HDMI.
- **Low Blue Light:** Reduces the amount of blue light from the screen to reduce eye strain.
