## Installation Instructions

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/installation-steps.png" alt="Screenmate Flip 14 inch installation steps" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

1. Carefully remove the Screenmate from the packaging.
2. Fold out the right screen in the direction shown in image 2.
3. Fold out the left screen as shown in image 3.
4. Rotate the left screen 180° in the direction indicated.
5. Place the Screenmate on a flat surface and open the adjustable stand for extra support (see image 5).
6. Connect the Screenmate to your laptop using the appropriate cables.

## Connection Options

The Screenmate Flip 14" supports two connection scenarios. Pick the one that matches the ports on your laptop.

### 1. Two USB-C Cables

If your laptop has two USB-C ports, use the two supplied USB-C cables to connect your laptop to the Screenmate.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-1-dual-usb-c.png" alt="Two USB-C cable connection" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 2. USB-C + HDMI + USB-A Cables

If your laptop has one USB-C port, one HDMI port and one USB-A port, you can connect the Screenmate as follows:

Use the supplied USB-C cable to connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop. Connect the other side using the HDMI cable together with the USB-C to USB-A cable.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-2-usb-c-hdmi-usb-a.png" alt="USB-C, HDMI and USB-A connection" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

<Note>
**Note:** The HDMI port does not supply power, so the Screenmate must be connected to a power source of 5V/2A or higher. The easiest way is via the USB-A port on your laptop.
</Note>

## Storing the Screenmate

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/storage-steps.png" alt="Screenmate Flip 14 inch storage steps" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

1. Disconnect the Screenmate from your laptop.
2. Rotate the left screen 180° back in the direction shown in image 1.
3. Fold both screens in as shown in image 2.
4. Place the Screenmate in the protective sleeve to prevent damage and store it in a safe place.
