## Display Configuration

### Windows <svg className="inline-block w-5 h-5 -mt-1 ml-2" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="currentColor"><path d="M0 3.449L9.75 2.1v9.451H0m10.949-9.602L24 0v11.4H10.949M0 12.6h9.75v9.451L0 20.699M10.949 12.6H24V24l-12.9-1.801"/></svg>

**Want to extend your workspace?**
Go to Display settings and choose **Extend desktop to this display**. Use the **Identify** button to see which screen has which number. You can then drag the screens into the correct order.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/display-windows.png" alt="Windows display settings with the Identify button" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

**Is a screen upside down?**
Select the affected screen, go to **Display orientation** and choose **Flipped** to correct it.

**Need more overview?**
Click **Scale** and set it to **150%** for larger text and elements.

**Working with three screens?**
Arrange all three displays in the **Identify** view by dragging each one to match its physical position.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/display-windows-scaling.png" alt="Windows scale, resolution and display orientation settings" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

1. Open **System Settings**.
2. Go to **Displays**.
3. Click **Arrange** as shown in image 1.
4. Drag the display to the desired side as shown in image 2.

## Sound Settings

### Windows <svg className="inline-block w-5 h-5 -mt-1 ml-2" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="currentColor"><path d="M0 3.449L9.75 2.1v9.451H0m10.949-9.602L24 0v11.4H10.949M0 12.6h9.75v9.451L0 20.699M10.949 12.6H24V24l-12.9-1.801"/></svg>

1. Click the speaker icon at the bottom-right of the taskbar.
2. On the right side of the volume bar, pick the desired output device.
3. You can adjust the volume per application individually.
4. At the very bottom you'll find a shortcut to the full sound settings.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/sound-windows.png" alt="Windows sound settings" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### macOS <svg className="inline-block w-5 h-5 -mt-1 ml-2" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="currentColor"><path d="M18.71 19.5c-.83 1.24-1.71 2.45-3.05 2.47-1.34.03-1.77-.79-3.29-.79-1.53 0-2 .77-3.27.82-1.31.05-2.3-1.32-3.14-2.53C4.25 17 2.94 12.45 4.7 9.39c.87-1.52 2.43-2.48 4.12-2.51 1.28-.02 2.5.87 3.29.87.78 0 2.26-1.07 3.81-.91.65.03 2.47.26 3.64 1.98-.09.06-2.17 1.28-2.15 3.81.03 3.02 2.65 4.03 2.68 4.04-.03.07-.42 1.44-1.38 2.83M13 3.5c.73-.83 1.94-1.46 2.94-1.5.13 1.17-.34 2.35-1.04 3.19-.69.85-1.83 1.51-2.95 1.42-.15-1.15.41-2.35 1.05-3.11z"/></svg>

1. Open the **Apple menu** and go to **System Settings**.
2. In the sidebar, click **Sound**.
3. Go to the **Output** tab.
4. Select the desired output device from the list (e.g. S6 or Speaker).

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/sound-macos.png" alt="macOS sound settings" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />
