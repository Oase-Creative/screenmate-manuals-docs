## Unfolding the Screens

Follow these steps in order to safely deploy both extension screens behind your laptop.

### 1. Release the screen from the stand

<Warning>
Unfold the monitor completely to avoid damage.
</Warning>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-1-unfold.png" alt="Release the screen from the stand" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 2. Attach the main stand

Place the small support and use the 360° rotatable mount to position it.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-2-main-stand.png" alt="Attach the main stand" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 3. Mount the screen support

<Warning>
Hold both red arrow positions with both hands and pull the screen outward to release the single screen.
</Warning>

You can use the Infinity Lite with a single screen support, in a vertical position, or in a horizontal position.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-3-screen-support.png" alt="Mount the screen support" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 4. Pull out the frame

Click open the frame to extend it.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-4-frame.png" alt="Pull out the frame" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 5. Adjust the stand

The stand has a 360° rotatable support.

<Warning>
Open the stand, set it to the correct support angle, place it behind your computer screen and finish the installation of the display stand.
</Warning>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-5-adjust.png" alt="Adjust the stand" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 6. Open the screens

<Note>
Adjust the distance between the portable screen and the computer screen to suit your preferences for a better user experience.
</Note>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-6-open.png" alt="Open the screens" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 7. Close the screens

<Warning>
Follow the correct sequence when opening and closing to avoid damage to the device. Store your Screenmate carefully to prevent damage to the equipment.
</Warning>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-7-close.png" alt="Close the screens" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

## Connection Options

The Infinity Lite supports two main connection methods depending on your laptop's ports.

### Connect the Screenmate Infinity Lite to your laptop

**USB-C connection**

If your laptop has a USB-C port with video support, you can use a single USB-C to USB-C cable to connect the product to your laptop. Plug the cable into the first or second port of the Screenmate and connect it to the USB-C port of your laptop.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-usb-c.png" alt="USB-C only connection" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

**USB and HDMI connection**

If your laptop has a USB-A or USB-C port **and** an HDMI port, use two cables:

- One HDMI to USB-C cable (for video)
- One USB-A or USB-C to USB-C cable (for power)

<Warning>
Connect the HDMI to USB-C cable to the **third** port of the Screenmate for the video signal. Connect the USB-A or USB-C to USB-C cable to the first or second port for power.
</Warning>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-usb-hdmi.png" alt="USB + HDMI connection" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

<Note>
**Important:** If the screen does not receive a signal or the signal is unstable, connect an external 5V/2A power adapter so your Screenmate has enough power.
</Note>

### Connect the Screenmate to your phone or other devices

Switch to the correct connection mode (external power required) to use the Screenmate with a phone, game console or other USB-C device. Phone mode also requires external power.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-phone-console.png" alt="Phone or console connection" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />
