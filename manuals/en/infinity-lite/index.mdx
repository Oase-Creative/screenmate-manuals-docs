## What is the Screenmate Infinity Lite?

The Screenmate Infinity Lite is a 15.6" Full HD dual-screen extension that mounts behind your laptop, giving you two extra portable displays for more screen real estate on the go. It connects via USB-C or HDMI and is designed for plug-and-play use with laptops, phones, tablets and game consoles.

| **Panel Type** | IPS |

| **Dimensions (folded)** | 36.1 × 21.6 × 4.5 cm |

<Note>
**Important Information:**

If the screen does not receive a signal or the signal is unstable, connect an external 5V/2A power adapter so your Screenmate has enough power. The required DC input is between 5V and 20V (with a tolerance of ±2V).
</Note>
