## Controls and OSD Menu

**Turn on:** Press and hold the power button to switch the monitor on. The monitor also turns on automatically when power is connected.

**Open the OSD menu:** Press the power button to open the OSD menu.

**Navigate:** Use the + or − button to navigate through the menu. The selected option is highlighted in yellow.

**Select:** Press the power button to select an option or confirm a setting.

**Adjust settings:** Use the + or − button to change a value and press the power button to save.

**Go back:** Press and hold the − button to return to the previous menu.

### Volume and Brightness Shortcuts

- Press the **+** button to adjust the brightness.
- Press the **−** button to adjust the volume.

### OSD Lock

If the front buttons stop responding, the OSD may be locked. Press and hold the button above the power button for **10 seconds** to unlock it.
