## Ports and Buttons

This section provides an overview of all physical ports and control buttons on the Screenmate One 4K.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20One%204K%20-%20Handleiding%20images/ports-buttons-diagram.png" alt="Screenmate One 4K ports and buttons overview" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

Use the USB-C port for power & video if your device has a USB-C connector with video output (DisplayPort Alt Mode). This single connection carries both power and video over one cable.

If your USB-C port doesn't support video, use the USB-C port for power in combination with the HDMI cable to carry the video signal.

### USB-C Port (Power & Video)

Carries both video and power when connected to a USB-C device with DisplayPort Alt Mode support.

### USB-C Port (Power)

Dedicated power input. Use with the supplied USB-C cable and power adapter.

### HDMI Port

Connect an external device via the supplied Mini HDMI to HDMI cable.

### 3.5mm Headphone Jack

For connecting headphones or external speakers.

### USB-A Port

For connecting USB 2.0 accessories such as a mouse or keyboard. When a signal is received via the USB-C port, the Screenmate automatically recognises connected accessories.

### Indicator Light

Status LED indicating power and signal state.

### Menu Button (Power / OSD)

Press and hold to switch the monitor on. When the monitor is on, press to open the OSD menu. See the [On-Screen Menu](/manuals/en/one-4k/osd) section for full details.

### + and − Buttons

- **+ button:** Navigate up in the OSD; opens the brightness shortcut menu in general mode.
- **− button:** Navigate down in the OSD; opens the volume shortcut menu in general mode. Press and hold to return to the previous menu.
