## What is the Screenmate One 4K?

The Screenmate One 4K is a 15.6" 4K UHD portable monitor with a built-in stand, designed to give you a high-resolution second screen for your laptop, phone, tablet, or game console. It connects via USB-C or HDMI and supports both video and power over a single USB-C cable on compatible devices.
