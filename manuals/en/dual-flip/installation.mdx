## Choose Your Cables

To work out which cables you need, first check which ports your laptop has. The Screenmate Dual Flip can be connected in either of the following ways:

- 2× USB-C
- 1× USB-C & 1× USB-A & 1× HDMI

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/port-icons.png" alt="USB-C, HDMI and USB-A port icons" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

## Installation Instructions

1. Carefully remove the Screenmate from the packaging.
2. Fold the right screen outward.
3. Fold the left screen outward.
4. Rotate the left screen 180° in the direction shown.
5. Place the Screenmate on a flat surface and fold out the adjustable stand for extra support.
6. Connect the Screenmate to your laptop using the appropriate cables.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-steps.png" alt="Six-step Dual Flip unfolding diagram" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

## Connection Options

### 1. Two USB-C cables

If your laptop has two USB-C ports, use the two included USB-C cables to connect your laptop to the Screenmate.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-1-dual-usb-c.png" alt="Dual USB-C connection to laptop" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 2. One USB-C cable, one HDMI cable, and one USB-A cable

If your laptop has one USB-C port, one HDMI port, and one USB-A port, you can connect the Screenmate as follows:

Use the included USB-C cable to connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop. Connect the other side using the HDMI cable together with the USB-C to USB-A cable.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-2-usb-c-hdmi-usb-a.png" alt="USB-C + HDMI + USB-A connection to laptop" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

<Note>
**Important:** The HDMI port does not supply power, so the Screenmate must be connected to a power source of 5V/2A or higher. This can easily be done via your laptop's USB-A port.
</Note>

## Storage

When you're done using the Dual Flip, store it as follows:

1. Disconnect the Screenmate from your laptop.
2. Rotate the left screen 180° back.
3. Fold both screens in.
4. Place the Screenmate in the protective case to prevent damage and store it in a safe place.
