## What is the Screenmate Dual Flip?

The Screenmate Dual Flip 16" is a foldable dual-screen extension that attaches to your laptop and unfolds into two extra 16" displays — one on each side. Each screen runs at 2560 × 1600 resolution and connects via USB-C or via USB-C + HDMI + USB-A.

<Note>
**Important Information:**

Take good care of your Screenmate Flip and do not press on the screens to prevent damage. Do not rotate the screens beyond the maximum angle shown below.

- **Left screen:** 0° – 245° (tilts 180° up and down)
- **Right screen:** 0° – 205°

The Screenmate uses a small amount of power in standby mode. For energy-efficient operation, we recommend disconnecting the power cable when the monitor is not in use.
</Note>
