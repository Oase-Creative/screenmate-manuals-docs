## Ports and Buttons

This section provides an overview of all physical ports and control buttons on the Screenmate Dual Flip.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/ports-buttons-diagram.png" alt="Screenmate Dual Flip ports and buttons overview" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### USB-C Port

Power supply and video transfer.

### USB-C Port

Power supply and video transfer.

### Mini HDMI

Video transfer (no power supply).

### M — OSD Menu Button

Press the **M** button to open the on-screen menu (OSD) and to confirm selections. Press and hold to return to the previous page.

### + Button (Increase Brightness)

Increases brightness in general use, and navigates / increases values inside the OSD menu.

### − Button (Decrease Brightness)

Decreases brightness in general use, and navigates / decreases values inside the OSD menu.
