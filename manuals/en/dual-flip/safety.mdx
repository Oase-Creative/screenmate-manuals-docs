## Safety Instructions

Please read the following guidelines carefully before using the monitor. They ensure safe operation and extend the lifespan of your Screenmate.

- Remove the protective film from the screen surface before use.
- Only use the included AC/DC adapter as power supply.
- Place the adapter in a well-ventilated area, away from heat sources.
- Make sure the power outlet is properly grounded and suitable for the correct amperage.
- The monitor operates on a DC input between 5V and 20V (with a tolerance of ±2V).
- Only use the device with a 5V power source via the appropriate cable.
- Prevent exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity to prevent damage to the electronics.
- Clean the screen only with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning agents.
- Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in humid environments.
- Do not drop the monitor and protect the screen from impacts, pressure or sharp objects.
- Keep ventilation openings clear and ensure sufficient air circulation to prevent overheating.
- Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or transmitting equipment to prevent interference.
- Recommended ambient temperature: between -20°C and 60°C.
- Turn off the screen when not in use.
