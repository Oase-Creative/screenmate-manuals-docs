## Display Configuration on Windows <svg className="inline-block w-5 h-5 -mt-1 ml-2" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="currentColor"><path d="M0 3.449L9.75 2.1v9.451H0m10.949-9.602L24 0v11.4H10.949M0 12.6h9.75v9.451L0 20.699M10.949 12.6H24V24l-12.9-1.801"/></svg>

### Want to extend your workspace?

Go to **Display settings** and choose **Extend desktop to this display**.

Use the **Identify** button to see which screen has which number. You can then drag the screens into the correct order.

### Is a screen upside down?

Select the screen, go to **Display orientation** and choose **Flipped** to correct it.

### Want a clearer overview?

Click on **Scale** and set it to 150% for a larger display of text and elements.

### Working with three screens?

Use the settings of your graphics card to synchronize the screens.
