## What is the Screenmate Flip 15.6"?

The Screenmate Flip 15.6" is a portable dual-screen add-on that attaches to your laptop, giving you two extra Full HD displays for more overview and focus. The screens fold neatly together so you can take your workspace anywhere.

## Package Contents

- **Screenmate Flip 15.6"** — the dual-screen unit
- **1x USB-C with USB-A adapter cable (90 cm)** — USB-C port: video and power · USB-A port: power only
- **2x USB-C to USB-C cables (60 cm)** — video and power
- **1x Mini HDMI to HDMI cable (90 cm)** — video only
- **Protective sleeve**

<Note>
**Important Information:**

Take good care of your Screenmate Flip and do not press on the screens to avoid damage. Do not rotate the screens beyond the maximum angles shown below.

- **Left** screen: 0° – 245° (180° tiltable up and down)
- **Right** screen: 0° – 205°

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Flip%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/warning-rotation.png" alt="Screen rotation angle limits" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200 mt-2" />

The Screenmate consumes a small amount of power in standby mode. For energy-efficient operation, we recommend disconnecting the power cable when the monitor is not in use.
