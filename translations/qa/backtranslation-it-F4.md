**Frontmatter title:** "Display settings"

### Windows display configuration

Right-click on the desktop and choose **Display settings** ('Impostazioni schermo') to open the display configuration.

**Image alt 1:** Right-click on the desktop and choose Display settings (Impostazioni schermo)
**Image alt 2:** Arrange the screens, then use Identify (Identifica) and Extend desktop to this display (Estendi il desktop a questo schermo)
**Image alt 3:** Scale and layout (Ridimensionamento e layout): adjust Scale (Ridimensionamento)

**Want to extend your workspace?**

Go to Display settings and choose 'Extend desktop to this display' ('Estendi il desktop a questo schermo'). Use the 'Identify' ('Identifica') button to see which screen has which number. Then you can easily drag the screens into the correct order.

**Video fallback text:** Your browser does not support the video tag.

**Screen upside down?**

Select the affected screen, go to 'Display orientation' ('Orientamento dello schermo') and choose 'Capovolto' ['Flipped'/'Upside down'] to correct the problem.

**Want more room on screen?**

Click on 'Scale' ('Ridimensionamento') and set it to 150% for a larger display of text and elements.

### macOS display configuration

1. Open System Settings.
2. Go to Displays.
3. Click on Arrangement.
4. Arrange the screens in the desired order.

**Video fallback text:** Your browser does not support the video tag.

**Screen upside down?**

Select the affected screen, go to 'Rotation' and choose 'Standard' to correct the problem.

### Choose the cables

First check which ports your laptop has. Use the icons below as a reference.

**Image alt (light):** USB-C, HDMI and USB-A port icons
**Image alt (dark):** USB-C, HDMI and USB-A port icons

You can connect the Screenmate in the following ways:

- 2 USB-C
- 1 USB-C and 1 USB-A and 1 HDMI
- 2 USB-A and 2 HDMI

### Protective cap

If your laptop's screen is less than 6 mm thick, use the protective cap so that the Screenmate stays firmly in place on the display.

**Image alt:** Protective cap fitted on the Screenmate's brackets

### Installation steps

1. Take the Screenmate out of the box.
2. Open the two screens in the direction shown in image 2.
3. Rest the bracket against the back of your laptop's screen and make sure it grips firmly, so the screen stays properly locked in place.
4. Lift the stand and pull it out while holding the button pressed. The stand is built into the back of the Screenmate and provides extra support. Press the button again to retract the stand.
5. Connect the right cables: plug one end into your laptop and the other into the Screenmate. See [Ports and buttons](/it/manuals/expand/controls) for an overview of all the available connections.

**Image alt:** Installation steps 1 through 6

### Connection options

The Screenmate Expand can be connected in three different ways. Choose the one that suits your laptop's ports.

#### 1. Two USB-C cables

If your laptop has two USB-C ports, use the two supplied USB-C cables to connect the laptop to the Screenmate. Each cable carries both video and power.

**Note callout:**
**Important:** make sure you use the correct USB-C port on the Screenmate.

**Image alt:** Two USB-C cables connecting the laptop to the Screenmate Expand

#### 2. 1 USB-C, 1 HDMI and 1 USB-A

If your laptop has one USB-C port, one HDMI port and one USB-A port, you can connect the Screenmate like this:

Use the supplied USB-C cable to connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop. Connect the other side with the HDMI cable and the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**Image alt:** USB-C, HDMI and USB-A connection to the Screenmate Expand

**Note callout:**
**Note:** The HDMI port does not supply power, so the Screenmate must be connected to a power source of 5 V/2 A or higher. Your laptop's USB-A port works perfectly well for this purpose.

#### 3. 2 USB-A and 2 HDMI

If your laptop does not have enough HDMI ports, use a compatible HDMI hub or adapter. This connection requires four cables plus an additional hub or adapter.

**Procedure:**

1. Connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop with an HDMI cable and a USB-C to USB-A cable.
2. Plug the HDMI hub or adapter into your laptop, then connect the other side of the Screenmate with a second HDMI cable and another USB-C to USB-A cable.

**Image alt:** Two HDMI connections and two USB-A connections to the Screenmate Expand via a hub

**Note callout:**
**Note:** The HDMI port does not supply power. Connect the Screenmate to a power source, for example via your laptop's USB-A port.

### Using the OSD

1. Press the **M (Menu)** button to open the OSD menu.
2. Use the **+ / −** buttons to select the setting you want.
3. Press **M (Menu)** to confirm the choice.
4. Press and hold **M (Menu)** to go back to the previous page.

### OSD settings

#### 1. Backlight

**Image alt:** Backlight OSD menu

- **Brightness (BRIGHTNESS):** adjusts the screen brightness (0–100).
- **Contrast (CONTRAST):** adjusts the screen contrast (0–100).
- **ECO mode (ECO):** preset picture modes (Standard, Game, Movie, Text, FPS, RTS, Energy Saving).
- **DCR (Dynamic Contrast Ratio):** turns dynamic contrast on or off (ON / OFF).
- **Sharpness (SHARPNESS):** adjusts the image sharpness (0–4).

#### 2. Image

**Image alt:** Image OSD menu

- **Format (ASPECT):** switches between **4:3** and **WIDE**.

#### 3. Color

**Image alt:** Color OSD menu

- **Color temperature (COLOR TEMP.):** set the RGB values manually or choose a preset mode (Warm, Cool, User, Standard).
- **Red (RED):** adjusts the red channel (0–100).
- **Green (GREEN):** adjusts the green channel (0–100).
- **Blue (BLUE):** adjusts the blue channel (0–100).

#### 4. OSD settings

**Image alt:** Settings OSD menu

- **Language (LANGUAGE):** choose the OSD language. Available languages: English, French, German, Simplified Chinese, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Polish, Dutch, Japanese, Korean.
- **OSD timer (OSD TIMER):** sets how long the settings menu stays on screen before closing automatically (10–60 seconds).
- **Transparency (TRANSPARENCY):** adjusts the transparency of the settings menu.

#### 5. Reset

**Image alt:** Reset OSD menu

- **Reset (RESET):** choose RESET to return all settings to the factory values.
- **HDR mode (HDR MODE):** turns on HDR (High Dynamic Range) if the device supports it. Available modes: Off, Auto, 2084.

#### 6. Other

**Image alt:** Other OSD menu

- **Source (SOURCE):** choose between two signal sources: Type-C1 / Type-C2 and HDMI.
- **Reduced blue light (LOW BLUE LIGHT):** reduces the amount of blue light emitted by the screen in order to reduce eye strain.

## it/manuals/expand/safety.mdx

**Frontmatter title:** "Safety instructions"
**Frontmatter description:** "Important safety information and warnings"

### Safety instructions

Read the following guidance carefully before using the monitor. It ensures safe operation and extends the life of your Screenmate.

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface before use.
2. Use only the supplied AC/DC power adapter as the power supply.
3. Place the power adapter in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure the power outlet is earthed and rated for the correct amperage.
5. The monitor operates on a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Use the device only with a 5 V power source via the appropriate cable.
7. Avoid exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity, to prevent damage to the electronic components.
8. Clean the screen only with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning agents.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor, and protect the screen from impact, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Do not block the ventilation openings and ensure sufficient air circulation, to prevent overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or to transmitting equipment, to prevent interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between -20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch the screen off when it is not in use.

## it/manuals/one-4k/installation.mdx

**Frontmatter title:** "Installation"
**Frontmatter description:** "Connecting your Screenmate One 4K"

### Getting started

- The Screenmate switches on automatically as soon as you connect it with the correct cables.
- Always use the supplied cables for optimal performance.
- The computer automatically recognizes the video signal and adjusts the display settings.
- If it does not work right away, manually select the correct input source in the device's display settings.
- If your device supports charging via USB-C, the screen charges automatically as soon as you connect the charger to the Screenmate.
- With Power Delivery (PD) support, the Screenmate automatically switches to fast-charging mode.
- When a signal is received via the USB-C port, the Screenmate also automatically recognizes connected USB 2.0 accessories, such as a mouse or a keyboard.

### Connection options

The Screenmate One 4K supports five connection scenarios. Choose the one that suits your device and the cables you have available.

#### 1. Laptop via USB-C cable

Connect the Screenmate to your laptop with the USB-C to USB-C cable. A single USB-C cable carries both the video signal and power.

**Note callout:**
**Important:** if the connected device does not supply enough power, the monitor must be powered separately via an additional power source.

**Image alt:** USB-C connection to the laptop

Need additional power? Use the USB-C to USB-A cable together with a power adapter:

**Image alt:** USB-C connection to the laptop with additional power

#### 2. Laptop via HDMI cable

Does your laptop not have a USB-C port? Connect the Screenmate via an HDMI cable for the video signal, together with a USB-C to USB-A cable for power.

The USB-A cable can also be plugged into a USB-A port on the laptop for power.

**Image alt:** HDMI connection to the laptop

#### 3. Phone or tablet via USB-C cable

With a single USB-C cable you can connect your phone or tablet directly to the Screenmate's USB-C port, for both video and power. Note: your phone or tablet must have a USB-C port with video support — not all USB-C ports can transmit video.

**Image alt:** USB-C connection to the phone or tablet

If the device does not supply enough power, power the monitor separately via an additional power source:

**Image alt:** USB-C connection to the phone or tablet with additional power

#### 4. USB-C games console

To use the Screenmate with a games console that has a USB-C connection, follow these steps:

**Power:** connect the supplied power adapter to a wall socket and connect it to the monitor with the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**Connecting the console:** use the USB-C to USB-C cable to connect the monitor's USB-C port to the console's USB-C port.

Once both connections have been made, the console's video signal appears automatically on the monitor.

**Image alt:** USB-C connection to a games console

#### 5. Devices with an HDMI port

To connect the Screenmate to an HDMI device — such as a PC, a laptop, an Xbox, a PlayStation 4/5 or a Nintendo Charging Dock (not the Nintendo Switch console itself) — follow these steps:

**Power:** connect the supplied power adapter to a wall socket and connect it to the monitor with the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**Connecting the HDMI device:** use the Mini-HDMI to HDMI cable to connect the monitor's Mini-HDMI port to the device's HDMI port.

Once both connections have been made, the HDMI device's video signal appears automatically on the monitor.

**Image alt:** HDMI connection to a PC, a console or a camera

### Safety instructions

Read the following guidance carefully before using the monitor. It ensures safe operation and extends the life of your Screenmate.

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface before use.
2. Use only the supplied AC/DC power adapter as the power supply.
3. Place the power adapter in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure the power outlet is earthed and rated for the correct amperage.
5. The monitor operates on a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Use the device only with a 5 V power source via the appropriate cable.
7. Avoid exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity, to prevent damage to the electronic components.
8. Clean the screen only with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning agents.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor, and protect the screen from impact, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Do not block the ventilation openings and ensure sufficient air circulation, to prevent overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or to transmitting equipment, to prevent interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between -20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch the screen off when it is not in use.
