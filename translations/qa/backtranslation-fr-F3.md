## fr/manuals/flip/safety.mdx

- description: "Important safety information and warnings"
- icon: "shield-exclamation"

### Safety instructions

Read the following instructions carefully before using the monitor. They guarantee safe use and extend the service life of your Screenmate.

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface before use.
2. Use only the supplied AC/DC adapter as the power supply.
3. Place the adapter in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure the power socket is properly earthed and suited to the required amperage.
5. The monitor works with a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Only use the device with a 5 V power source via the appropriate cable.
7. Avoid any exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity, in order to avoid any damage to the electronic components.
8. Clean the screen only with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning products.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor and protect the screen against impacts, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Keep the ventilation openings clear and ensure sufficient air circulation, in order to avoid any overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or transmitting equipment, in order to avoid interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between -20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch off the screen when it is not in use.

### Using the OSD menu

1. Press the **M** (Menu) button to open the OSD menu.
2. Use the **+** and **−** buttons to select the desired setting.
3. Press the **M** (Menu) button to confirm your choice.
4. Press and hold the **M** (Menu) button to return to the previous page.

**Image alt:** OSD Backlight menu

### Backlight

- **Brightness (BRIGHTNESS):** adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Contrast (CONTRAST):** adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **ECO mode (ECO):** preset picture modes for different uses (Standard, Game, Movie, Text, FPS, RTS, Energy Saving).
- **DCR (dynamic contrast ratio):** choose ON or OFF to enable or disable dynamic contrast.
- **Sharpness (SHARPNESS):** adjustable from 0 to 4.

### Image

**Image alt:** OSD Image menu

- **Aspect ratio (ASPECT):** switch the aspect ratio between 4:3 and WIDE.

### Colour

**Image alt:** OSD Colour menu

- **Colour temperature (COLOR TEMP.):** set the RGB values manually or choose a preset mode (Warm, Cool, User, Standard).
- **Red (RED):** adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Green (GREEN):** adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Blue (BLUE):** adjustable from 0 to 100.

### Settings

**Image alt:** OSD Settings menu

- **Language (LANGUAGE):** choose the OSD language. Available languages: English, French, German, Simplified Chinese, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Polish, Dutch, Japanese, Korean.
- **OSD timer (OSD TIMER):** set the delay after which the settings menu closes automatically (10–60 seconds).
- **Transparency (TRANSPARENCY):** adjust the transparency of the settings menu.

### Reset

**Image alt:** OSD Reset menu

- **Reset (RESET):** choose RESET to restore all factory settings.
- **HDR mode (HDR MODE):** enable HDR (High Dynamic Range) if the device is compatible. Available modes: Off, Auto, 2084.

### Other

**Image alt:** OSD Other menu

- **Source (SOURCE):** choose from two signal sources: Type-C1 / Type-C2 and HDMI.
- **Low blue light (LOW BLUE LIGHT):** reduces the amount of blue light emitted by the screen in order to limit eye strain.
