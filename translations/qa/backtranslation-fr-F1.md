### Safety instructions

Read the following instructions carefully before using the monitor. They guarantee safe use and extend the service life of your Screenmate.

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface before use.
2. Use only the supplied AC/DC adapter as the power supply.
3. Place the adapter in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure the power outlet is correctly earthed and suitable for the required amperage.
5. The monitor operates on a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Only use the device with a 5 V power source via the appropriate cable.
7. Avoid any exposure to humidity, dust and static electricity, in order to avoid damage to the electronic components.
8. Clean the screen only with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning products.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor and protect the screen against impacts, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Keep the ventilation openings clear and ensure sufficient air circulation, in order to avoid overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or transmitting equipment, in order to avoid interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between -20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch the screen off when it is not in use.

### Charging the Screenmate OneCable

When the Screenmate is connected to an external power source, such as your laptop's USB-C mains adapter, and your laptop is connected via a USB-C port with Power Delivery, reverse charging mode switches on automatically.

## fr/manuals/onecable/troubleshooting.mdx

**title:** "FAQ"
**description:** "Frequently asked questions and troubleshooting"

### FAQ

#### My screen stays black and no image appears after plugging in. What should I do?

Check whether the indicator light on the motherboard is lit. Make sure both ends of the connection cable are correctly plugged in. Check whether your laptop delivers sufficient power to supply the Screenmate.

#### The screen is unstable, flickers or cuts out at times. What can be the cause?

Check whether the laptop delivers sufficient power. If necessary, connect an external power supply, such as a USB-C laptop charger, a 5 V/3 A DC adapter or a PD charger.

#### My laptop has no USB-C port. Can I still use the Screenmate?

Use the dual USB-A to USB-C cable:

Plug the black USB-A plug into a USB-A port on the laptop (for data). Plug the red USB-A plug into another USB-A port (for power).

Does your laptop have only one USB-A port?

Plug the black USB-A plug into the laptop. Plug the red USB-A plug into a charger or an external power source (for example a 5 V/3 A DC adapter or a PD charger). Then connect the USB-C end of the cable to the Screenmate.

#### A start-up logo appears, but no image is shown on my MacBook. What should I do?

Check whether the driver icon is visible in the menu bar, at the top right of your Mac. Do you not see the icon? Open the Launchpad on your Mac, look for the driver and launch it manually.

#### Can the Screenmate charge my laptop when it is plugged into a power source?

That is possible, provided the following conditions are met:

Your laptop's USB-C port supports Power Delivery (PD). The connection is made with a USB-C cable. The power supply is PD-compatible.

Connection method: connect your laptop's USB-C port to the Screenmate using a USB-C cable. Plug your laptop's charger into the Screenmate's USB-C Power port in order to activate reverse charging and automatically improve the screen brightness. Does your laptop charger not have a USB-C output? Then use a PD charger of at least 45 W.

### Connection options

The Infinity Lite supports two main connection methods, depending on your laptop's ports.

#### Connecting the Screenmate Infinity Lite to your laptop

**USB-C connection**

If your laptop has a USB-C port that supports video, a single USB-C to USB-C cable is enough to connect the product to your laptop. Plug the cable into the first or the second port of the Screenmate, then connect it to the USB-C port of your laptop.

**Image alt:** USB-C only connection

**USB and HDMI connection**

If your laptop has a USB-A or USB-C port **and** an HDMI port, use two cables:

- An HDMI to USB-C cable (for video)
- A USB-A or USB-C to USB-C cable (for power)

**Warning callout:**
Plug the HDMI to USB-C cable into the **third** port of the Screenmate for the video signal. Plug the USB-A or USB-C to USB-C cable into the first or the second port for power.

**Image alt:** USB + HDMI connection

**Note callout:**
**Important:** if the screen receives no signal or the signal is unstable, plug in an external 5 V/2 A mains adapter so that your Screenmate has sufficient power.
