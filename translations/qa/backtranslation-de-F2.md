## Connection options

The Screenmate Lite can be connected in five ways. Choose the variant that suits your device and the available cables.

### 1. Laptop via USB-C cable

Connect the Screenmate to your laptop with the USB-C-to-USB-C cable. A single USB-C cable carries both the video signal and the power.

**Note callout**
**Important:** If the connected device does not supply enough power, the monitor must be powered separately via an additional power source.

**Image alt:** "USB-C connection with the laptop"

Do you need more power? Then use the USB-C-to-USB-A cable together with a power adapter:

**Image alt:** "USB-C connection with the laptop and additional power supply"

### 2. Laptop via HDMI cable

Your laptop has no USB-C port? Connect the Screenmate via an HDMI cable for the video signal and additionally via a USB-C-to-USB-A cable for the power supply.

You can also connect the USB-A cable to a USB-A port on the laptop for the power supply.

**Image alt:** "HDMI connection with the laptop"

### 3. Smartphone or tablet via USB-C cable

With a single USB-C cable you can connect your smartphone or tablet directly to the Screenmate's USB-C port – for video signal and power supply at the same time. Note: your smartphone or tablet must have a USB-C port with video support – not every USB-C port can output video.

**Image alt:** "USB-C connection with smartphone or tablet"

If the device does not supply enough power, power the monitor separately via an additional power source:

**Image alt:** "USB-C connection with smartphone or tablet and additional power supply"

### 4. Games console via USB-C

To use the Screenmate with a games console that has a USB-C port, proceed as follows:

**Power supply:** Connect the supplied power adapter to a wall socket and connect it to the monitor via the USB-C-to-USB-A cable.

**Connecting the console:** Using the USB-C-to-USB-C cable, connect the monitor's USB-C port to the console's USB-C port.

As soon as both connections are made, the console's video signal appears automatically on the monitor.

**Image alt:** "USB-C connection with a games console"

### 5. Devices with an HDMI port

To connect the Screenmate to an HDMI device – such as a PC, a laptop, an Xbox, a PlayStation 4/5 or a Nintendo Charging Dock (not the Nintendo Switch itself) – proceed as follows:

**Power supply:** Connect the supplied power adapter to a wall socket and connect it to the monitor via the USB-C-to-USB-A cable.

**Connecting the HDMI device:** Using the Mini-HDMI-to-HDMI cable, connect the monitor's Mini-HDMI port to the device's HDMI port.

As soon as both connections are made, the HDMI device's video signal appears automatically on the monitor.

**Image alt:** "HDMI connection with a PC, a console or a camera"

- **Black level (0–100):** Set the black level in order to get deeper blacks and more detail in dark scenes.
- **Sharpness (0–4):** Increase the sharpness to make the image appear clearer and richer in detail.

**Image alt:** "OSD menu Image settings"

- **ECO mode:** Choose an image mode according to how you use the monitor:
  - **Standard:** Standard image mode.
  - **RTS:** Optimized for real-time strategy games (RTS).
  - **FPS:** Optimized for first-person shooter games (FPS).
  - **Text:** Optimal legibility of text.
  - **Movie:** Suitable for watching films.
  - **Game:** Optimized for games.

### 3. Color settings

**Image alt:** "OSD menu Color temperature"

- **Red (0–100):** Adjust the brightness of the red RGB value.
- **Green (0–100):** Adjust the brightness of the green RGB value.
- **Blue (0–100):** Adjust the brightness of the blue RGB value.

### 4. OSD settings

**Image alt:** "OSD menu Settings"

- **Language:** Choose from 12 available languages for the display of the OSD menu.
- **OSD timer (10–60 seconds):** Set how long the OSD menu stays visible on the screen.

## Safety instructions

Read the following instructions carefully before you use the monitor. These instructions ensure safe operation and extend the service life of your Screenmate.

1. Before use, remove the protective film from the screen surface.
2. Use only the supplied AC/DC power adapter as the power supply.
3. Place the power adapter in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure the wall socket is properly earthed and suitable for the correct current rating.
5. The monitor works with a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Use the device only with a 5 V power source via the appropriate cable.
7. Avoid exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity in order to prevent damage to the electronics.
8. Clean the screen only with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning agents.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor and protect the screen from impacts, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Keep the ventilation openings clear and ensure sufficient air circulation in order to prevent overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or transmitter installations in order to prevent interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between −20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch the screen off when you are not using it.

## Installing the display driver

Before you connect the monitor to your PC or laptop for the first time, you must install the appropriate display driver. The Screenmate Panorama needs this driver in order to drive several screens over a single cable.

Choose the software that matches your laptop's operating system. Download the file, unpack the archive and install the program by following the steps on the screen.

Download the driver from Silicon Motion's download page:
[https://www.siliconmotion.com/downloads/index.html](https://www.siliconmotion.com/downloads/index.html)

**Image alt:** "Overview of the available drivers for Windows and macOS"

## Connection options

### Option 1 – USB-C (only one cable)

For laptops with a USB-C port that supports video output (DisplayPort Alt Mode).

1. Connect the 65 W power adapter to the monitor's USB-C charging port with the supplied USB-C-to-USB-C cable.
2. Then connect the monitor to your laptop's USB-C port via a second USB-C-to-USB-C cable.

**Info callout**
Use the long white cable for the power and the short black cable to connect the Panorama to your laptop.

**Note callout**
A laptop's USB-C port alone does not supply enough power to run the Panorama at full brightness. For stable operation, always connect the 65 W power adapter.

**Note callout**
The Panorama can charge your laptop via USB-C (reverse charging, i.e. charging the other way round). If you additionally connect your laptop to a separate charger while the Panorama is also supplying it with power, this can lead to interference/malfunctions.

**Image alt:** "USB-C connection diagram: laptop, Screenmate Panorama and power adapter"

### Option 2 – USB-A + HDMI

For laptops whose USB-C port does not support video output. An additional HDMI cable is needed to drive all three screens.

1. Connect the 65 W power adapter to the monitor via the USB-C-to-USB-C charging cable.
2. Then connect the monitor to a USB-A port on your laptop via a USB-A-to-USB-C cable.
3. Finally, connect your laptop's HDMI port to the monitor via the Mini-HDMI-to-HDMI cable. On the monitor, plug the HDMI cable into the HDMI port next to the white power cable.

**Image alt:** "USB-A + HDMI connection diagram: laptop, Screenmate Panorama and power adapter"

## Settings per screen

The Screenmate Panorama offers separate settings for each screen. Follow the steps below to set up each screen.

### Opening the OSD menu

- Briefly press the **Power button** to open the OSD menu of a screen.
- Briefly press the Power button again to switch to the next screen.
- Use the **+ / −** buttons to navigate through the menus.
- Press the **Confirm/Exit button** to select an option.
- Use the **+ / −** buttons again to adjust values.

### Adjusting brightness

The brightness can be set per screen, from 0 to 100.

- Press the **Down (−) button** to open the brightness menu.
- Press the **Exit button** to close the menu.
- Press the **Down (−) button** again to open the brightness menu of the next screen.
- Adjust the brightness with the **+ / −** buttons.

### Adjusting volume

Volume control is only available on the left screen (DP monitor).

- Press the **Up (+) button** to open the volume menu.
- Set the volume from 0 to 100 with the **+ / −** buttons.

## Screen configuration (operating system)

The Panorama drives three independent screens. For that reason you may want to adjust the arrangement of the desktop in your operating system.

### Windows

**Extend the working surface:** Open the display settings and select **"Extend desktop to this display"**. With the **Identify** button you see which screen has which number. Then drag the screens into the correct order.

**Screen upside down?** Select the affected screen, go to **Display orientation** and select **"Landscape (flipped)"** to correct this.

**Do you need more space?** Click on **Scaling** and set it to 150% so that text and controls are shown larger.

**Image alt:** "Windows display settings: arranging screens and adjusting scaling"

1. Open the **System Settings**.
2. Go to **Displays**.
3. Click on **Arrange**, as shown in figure 1.
