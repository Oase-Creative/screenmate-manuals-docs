## Connection options

The Screenmate Lite supports five connection scenarios. Choose the one that matches your device and the cables you have available.

### 1. Laptop via USB-C cable

Connect the Screenmate to your laptop using the USB-C to USB-C cable. A single USB-C cable carries both the video signal and the power.

Note callout:
**Important:** if the connected device does not supply sufficient power, the monitor must be powered separately from an additional power source.

Image alt: "USB-C connection to the laptop"

Need additional power? Then use the USB-C to USB-A cable with a mains adapter:

Image alt: "USB-C connection to the laptop with additional power"

### 2. Laptop via HDMI cable

Does your laptop not have a USB-C port? Connect the Screenmate using an HDMI cable for the video signal, combined with a USB-C to USB-A cable for the power.

The USB-A cable can also be plugged into a USB-A port on the laptop for power.

Image alt: "HDMI connection to the laptop"

### 3. Phone or tablet via USB-C cable

With a single USB-C cable you can connect your phone or your tablet directly to the USB-C port of the Screenmate, for the video signal as well as for the power. Note: your phone or your tablet must have a USB-C port that supports video — not all USB-C ports can transmit a video signal.

Image alt: "USB-C connection to the phone or the tablet"

If the device does not supply sufficient power, power the monitor separately from an additional power source:

Image alt: "USB-C connection to the phone or the tablet with additional power"

### 4. USB-C game console

To use the Screenmate with a game console that has a USB-C connection, follow these steps:

**Power:** plug the supplied mains adapter into a power socket and connect it to the monitor using the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**Console connection:** use the USB-C to USB-C cable to link the USB-C port of the monitor to the USB-C port of the console.

Once both connections are made, the video signal from the console appears automatically on the monitor.

Image alt: "USB-C connection to a game console"

### 5. Devices with an HDMI port

To connect the Screenmate to an HDMI device — such as a PC, a laptop, an Xbox, a PlayStation 4/5 or a Nintendo Charging Dock (and not the Nintendo Switch console itself) — follow these steps:

**Power:** plug the supplied mains adapter into a power socket and connect it to the monitor using the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**HDMI device connection:** use the Mini-HDMI to HDMI cable to link the Mini-HDMI port of the monitor to the HDMI port of the device.

Once both connections are made, the video signal from the HDMI device appears automatically on the monitor.

Image alt: "HDMI connection to a PC, a console or a camera"

## On-screen menu settings

Press the power and back button to open the on-screen menu. Use the wheel to navigate, then press the button again to confirm a selection.

### 1. Brightness

Image alt: "OSD menu Brightness"

- **Brightness (0–100):** adjust the brightness of the screen.
- **Contrast (0–100):** change the contrast to optimise the difference between the light areas and the dark areas.
- **Black level (0–100):** adjust the black level to obtain deeper blacks and more detail in dark scenes.
- **Sharpness (0–4):** increase the sharpness to improve the clarity and the detail of the image.

### 2. Image modes

Image alt: "OSD menu Image"

- **ECO modes:** choose an image mode according to your use of the monitor:
  - **Standard:** default image mode.
  - **RTS:** optimised for Real-Time Strategy (RTS) games.
  - **FPS:** optimised for First-Person Shooter (FPS) games.
  - **Text:** optimal readability for text.
  - **Movie:** suited to watching films.
  - **Game:** optimised for games.
- **DCR (dynamic contrast ratio) (ON/OFF):** automatically adjusts the brightness and the contrast for better image quality.
- **Aspect ratio (16:9 / 4:3):** switch the aspect ratio between widescreen (16:9) and 4:3 depending on the content.

### 3. Colour settings

Image alt: "OSD menu Colour temperature"

- **Colour temperature:** choose User, Warm or Cool to adjust the overall intensity of the colours.
- **Red (0–100):** adjust the brightness of the red RGB value.
- **Green (0–100):** adjust the brightness of the green RGB value.
- **Blue (0–100):** adjust the brightness of the blue RGB value.

### 4. OSD settings

Image alt: "OSD menu Settings"

- **Language:** choose from the 12 available languages for the display of the OSD menu.
- **OSD timer (10–60 seconds):** set how long the OSD menu stays visible on the screen.
- **Transparency (0–100):** adjust the transparency of the OSD menu for better visibility.

**Frontmatter description:** "Important safety information and warnings"

## Safety instructions

Read the following instructions carefully before using the monitor. They guarantee safe use and extend the service life of your Screenmate.

1. Remove the protective film from the surface of the screen before use.
2. Use only the supplied AC/DC adapter as the power supply.
3. Place the adapter in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure the power socket is correctly earthed and suitable for the required amperage.
5. The monitor works with a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Only use the device with a 5 V power source via the appropriate cable.
7. Avoid any exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity in order to prevent damage to the electronic components.
8. Clean the screen only with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning products.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor and protect the screen against impacts, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Keep the ventilation openings clear and ensure sufficient air circulation in order to prevent overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or to transmitting equipment in order to avoid interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between -20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch the screen off when it is not in use.

## Installing the display driver

Before connecting the monitor to your PC or your laptop for the first time, you must install the appropriate display driver. The Screenmate Panorama needs this driver in order to drive multiple screens over a single cable.

Choose the software that matches your laptop's operating system, download the file, unpack the archive, then install the application by following the steps shown on screen.

Download the driver from the Silicon Motion download page:

Image alt: "Overview of the drivers available for Windows and macOS"

## Connection options

### Option 1 — USB-C (single cable)

For laptops fitted with a USB-C port that supports video output (DisplayPort Alt Mode).

1. Plug the 65 W mains adapter into the USB-C charging port of the monitor using the supplied USB-C to USB-C cable.
2. Plug the monitor into the USB-C port of your laptop using a second USB-C to USB-C cable.

Info callout:
Use the long white cable for the power, and the short black cable to connect the Panorama to your laptop.

Note callout:
The USB-C port of a laptop does not on its own supply enough power to run the Panorama at its maximum brightness. Always plug in the 65 W adapter for stable operation.

Note callout:
The Panorama can recharge your laptop over USB-C thanks to reverse charging. Plugging a separate charger into your laptop while the Panorama is also powering it can cause interference.

Image alt: "USB-C connection diagram: laptop, Screenmate Panorama and mains adapter"

### Option 2 — USB-A + HDMI

For laptops whose USB-C port does not support video output. An additional HDMI cable is needed to drive the three screens.

1. Plug the 65 W mains adapter into the monitor using the USB-C to USB-C charging cable.
2. Plug the monitor into a USB-A port on your laptop using a USB-A to USB-C cable.
3. Finally, link the HDMI port of your laptop to the monitor using the Mini-HDMI to HDMI cable. On the monitor, plug the HDMI cable into the HDMI port located next to the white power cable.

Image alt: "USB-A + HDMI connection diagram: laptop, Screenmate Panorama and mains adapter"

## Per-screen settings

The Screenmate Panorama offers independent settings for each screen. Follow the steps below to configure each of them.

### Opening the OSD menu

- Briefly press the **power button** to open the OSD menu of one screen.
- Briefly press the power button again to move to the next screen.
- Use the **+ / −** buttons to navigate the menus.
- Press the **Confirm / Exit** button to select an option.
- Use the **+ / −** buttons again to adjust the values.

### Adjusting the brightness

The brightness is set for each screen separately, from 0 to 100.

- Press the **Down (−)** button to open the brightness menu.
- Press the **Exit** button to close the menu.
- Press the **Down (−)** button again to open the brightness menu of the next screen.
- Adjust the brightness using the **+ / −** buttons.

### Adjusting the volume

The volume setting is only available on the left-hand screen (DP monitor).

- Press the **Up (+)** button to open the volume menu.
- Use the **+ / −** buttons to set the volume from 0 to 100.

## Display configuration (operating system)

The Panorama drives three independent screens, so you may want to adapt the Desktop layout in your operating system.

### Windows

**Extending your workspace:** open the Display Settings and choose **"Extend the Desktop to this display"**. Use the **Identify** button to see which screen has which number, then drag them into the right order.

**Screen upside down?** Select the screen concerned, go to **Display orientation** and choose **"Landscape (flipped)"** to correct this.

**Need more room?** Click **Scale** and set it to 150% to enlarge the text and the interface elements.
