## Safety instructions

Read the following guidance carefully before using the monitor. It ensures safe operation and extends the life of your Screenmate.

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface before use.
2. Use only the supplied AC/DC power adapter as the power source.
3. Place the power adapter in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure the power socket is earthed and suitable for the correct amperage.
5. The monitor works with a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Use the device only with a 5 V power source via the appropriate cable.
7. Avoid exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity to prevent damage to the electronic components.
8. Clean the screen only with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning agents.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor and protect the screen from impacts, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Do not block the ventilation openings and ensure sufficient air circulation to prevent overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or to transmitting equipment to avoid interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between -20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch the screen off when it is not in use.

*(Note: item 5 says 5–20 V DC input, while item 6 says to use only a 5 V power source — the tension is present in the Italian and is left as-is.)*

## Connection options

The Screenmate Lite supports five connection scenarios. Choose the one that suits your device and the cables you have available.

### 1. Laptop via USB-C cable

Connect the Screenmate to your laptop with the USB-C to USB-C cable. A single USB-C cable carries both the video signal and the power.

**Note callout**
**Important:** if the connected device does not supply enough power, the monitor must be powered separately via an additional power source.

**Image** — alt: "USB-C connection to the laptop"

Need extra power? Use the USB-C to USB-A cable together with a power adapter:

**Image** — alt: "USB-C connection to the laptop with additional power"

### 2. Laptop via HDMI cable

Does your laptop not have a USB-C port? Connect the Screenmate via an HDMI cable for the video signal, together with a USB-C to USB-A cable for the power.

The USB-A cable can also be plugged into a USB-A port on the laptop for power.

**Image** — alt: "HDMI connection to the laptop"

### 3. Phone or tablet via USB-C cable

With a single USB-C cable you can connect your phone or tablet directly to the Screenmate's USB-C port, both for video and for power. Note: your phone or tablet must have a USB-C port with video support — not all USB-C ports can carry video.

**Image** — alt: "USB-C connection to the phone or tablet"

If the device does not supply enough power, power the monitor separately via an additional power source:

**Image** — alt: "USB-C connection to the phone or tablet with additional power"

### 4. USB-C games console

To use the Screenmate with a games console that has a USB-C connection, follow these steps:

**Power:** connect the supplied power adapter to a wall socket and connect it to the monitor with the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**Connecting the console:** use the USB-C to USB-C cable to connect the monitor's USB-C port to the console's USB-C port.

Once both connections have been made, the console's video signal appears on the monitor automatically.

**Image** — alt: "USB-C connection to a games console"

### 5. Devices with an HDMI port

To connect the Screenmate to an HDMI device — such as a PC, a laptop, an Xbox, a PlayStation 4/5 or a Nintendo Charging Dock (not the Nintendo Switch console itself) — follow these steps:

**Power:** connect the supplied power adapter to a wall socket and connect it to the monitor with the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**Connecting the HDMI device:** use the Mini-HDMI to HDMI cable to connect the monitor's Mini-HDMI port to the device's HDMI port.

Once both connections have been made, the HDMI device's video signal appears on the monitor automatically.

**Image** — alt: "HDMI connection to a PC, a console or a camera"

## On-screen menu settings

Press the power and return button to open the on-screen menu. Use the scroll wheel to navigate and press the button again to confirm a selection.

### 1. Brightness

**Image** — alt: "OSD Brightness menu"

- **Brightness (0–100):** adjusts the screen brightness.
- **Contrast (0–100):** changes the contrast to optimise the difference between light and dark areas.
- **Black level (0–100):** sets the black level for deeper blacks and more detail in dark scenes.
- **Sharpness (0–4):** increases sharpness to improve the definition and detail of the image.

### 2. Picture mode

**Image** — alt: "OSD Image menu"

- **ECO mode:** choose a picture mode according to how you use the monitor:
  - **Standard:** the default picture mode.
  - **RTS:** optimised for Real-Time Strategy (RTS) games.
  - **FPS:** optimised for First-Person Shooter (FPS) games.
  - **Text:** optimal readability for text.
  - **Movie:** ideal for watching films.
  - **Game:** optimised for gaming.
- **DCR (Dynamic Contrast Ratio) (ON/OFF):** automatically adjusts brightness and contrast for better image quality.
- **Aspect ratio (16:9 / 4:3):** switches the aspect ratio between widescreen (16:9) and 4:3 depending on the content.

### 3. Colour settings

**Image** — alt: "OSD Colour temperature menu"

- **Colour temperature:** choose User, Warm or Cool to adjust the overall colour intensity.
- **Red (0–100):** adjusts the brightness of the red RGB value.
- **Green (0–100):** adjusts the brightness of the green RGB value.
- **Blue (0–100):** adjusts the brightness of the blue RGB value.

### 4. OSD settings

**Image** — alt: "OSD Settings menu"

- **Language:** choose from 12 available languages for the display of the OSD menu.
- **OSD timer (10–60 seconds):** sets how long the OSD menu stays visible on the screen.
- **Transparency (0–100):** adjusts the transparency of the OSD menu for a better view.

## Install the display driver

Before connecting the monitor to your PC or laptop for the first time, you must install the correct display driver. The Screenmate Panorama needs this driver in order to drive multiple screens over a single cable.

Choose the software that suits your laptop's operating system, download the file, extract the archive and install the application by following the on-screen instructions.

Download the driver from Silicon Motion's download page:

## Connection options

### Option 1 — USB-C (single cable)

For laptops with a USB-C port that supports video output (DisplayPort Alt Mode).

1. Connect the 65 W power adapter to the monitor's USB-C charging port with the supplied USB-C to USB-C cable.
2. Connect the monitor to your laptop's USB-C port with a second USB-C to USB-C cable.

**Info callout**
Use the long white cable for power and the short black cable to connect the Panorama to your laptop.

**Note callout**
A laptop's USB-C port on its own does not supply enough power to run the Panorama at maximum brightness. Always connect the 65 W power adapter for stable operation.

**Note callout**
The Panorama can charge your laptop via USB-C thanks to reverse charging. Connecting a separate charger to the laptop while the Panorama is also supplying power may cause interference.

**Image** — alt: "USB-C connection diagram: laptop, Screenmate Panorama and power adapter"

### Option 2 — USB-A + HDMI

For laptops whose USB-C port does not support video output. An additional HDMI cable is needed in order to drive all three screens.

1. Connect the 65 W power adapter to the monitor with the USB-C to USB-C charging cable.
2. Connect the monitor to a USB-A port on your laptop with a USB-A to USB-C cable.
3. Finally, connect your laptop's HDMI port to the monitor with the Mini-HDMI to HDMI cable. On the monitor, plug the HDMI cable into the HDMI port next to the white power cable.

**Image** — alt: "USB-A + HDMI connection diagram: laptop, Screenmate Panorama and power adapter"

## Per-screen settings

The Screenmate Panorama offers independent settings for each screen. Follow the steps below to configure each display.

### Opening the OSD menu

- Press the **power button** briefly to open the OSD menu of one screen.
- Press the power button briefly again to move to the next screen.
- Use the **+ / −** buttons to navigate in the menus.
- Press the **Confirm / Exit** button to select an option.
- Use the **+ / −** buttons again to adjust the values.

### Adjusting the brightness

Brightness is adjustable for each screen, from 0 to 100.

- Press the **Down (−)** button to open the brightness menu.
- Press the **Exit** button to close the menu.
- Press the **Down (−)** button again to open the brightness menu of the next screen.
- Adjust the brightness with the **+ / −** buttons.

### Adjusting the volume

Volume adjustment is available only on the left-hand screen (DP monitor).

- Press the **Up (+)** button to open the volume menu.
- Use the **+ / −** buttons to set the volume from 0 to 100.

## Display configuration (at operating-system level)

The Panorama drives three independent screens, so you may want to change the desktop layout in your operating system.

### Windows

**Extend your workspace:** open Display Settings and choose **"Extend the desktop to this display"**. Use the **Identify** button to see which number corresponds to each screen, then drag them into the correct order.

**Screen upside down?** Select the screen concerned, go to **Display orientation** and choose **"Flipped"** to correct it.
