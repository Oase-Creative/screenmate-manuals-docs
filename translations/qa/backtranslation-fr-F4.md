## fr/manuals/expand/safety.mdx

**Frontmatter title:** "Safety instructions"
**Frontmatter description:** "Important safety information and warnings"

### Safety instructions

Read the following instructions carefully before using the monitor. They ensure safe use and extend the lifespan of your Screenmate.

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface before use.
2. Use only the supplied AC/DC adapter as the power supply.
3. Place the adapter in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure the power socket is properly earthed and suitable for the required amperage.
5. The monitor operates on a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Only use the device with a 5 V power source via the appropriate cable.
7. Avoid any exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity, in order to avoid damage to the electronic components.
8. Clean the screen only with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning products.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor and protect the screen against knocks, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Keep the ventilation openings clear and ensure sufficient air circulation, in order to avoid overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or transmitting equipment, in order to avoid interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between -20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch the screen off when it is not in use.

### Choosing your cables

The Screenmate can be connected in the following ways:

- 2 USB-C
- 1 USB-C, 1 USB-A and 1 HDMI
- 2 USB-A and 2 HDMI

### Protective cap

If your laptop screen is less than 6 mm thick, use the protective cap so that the Screenmate fits perfectly onto your screen.

*Image alt:* Protective cap fitted onto the Screenmate's mounting brackets

### Installation steps

1. Unpack the Screenmate.
2. Unfold both screens in the direction shown in image 2.
3. Place the mounting bracket against the back of your laptop screen and make sure it holds firmly, so that your screen is gripped tightly.
4. Lift the stand and extend it while keeping the button pressed. The stand is built into the back of the Screenmate and provides extra support. Press the button again to retract the stand.
5. Plug in the right cables: connect one end to your laptop and the other to the Screenmate. See [Ports and buttons](/fr/manuals/expand/controls) for an overview of all available connections.

*Image alt:* Installation steps 1 to 6

### Connection options

The Screenmate Expand supports three connection scenarios. Choose the one that matches your laptop's ports.

#### 1. Two USB-C cables

If your laptop has two USB-C ports, use the two supplied USB-C cables to connect your laptop to the Screenmate. Each cable carries both video and power.

> **Note callout:**
> **Important:** make sure you use the correct USB-C port on your Screenmate.

*Image alt:* Two USB-C cables connecting the laptop to the Screenmate Expand

#### 2. 1 USB-C, 1 HDMI and 1 USB-A

If your laptop has a USB-C port, an HDMI port and a USB-A port, you can connect the Screenmate as follows:

Use the supplied USB-C cable to connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop. Connect the other side with the HDMI cable and the USB-C to USB-A cable.

*Image alt:* USB-C, HDMI and USB-A connection to the Screenmate Expand

> **Note callout:**
> **Note:** the HDMI port does not supply power; the Screenmate must therefore be connected to a power source of 5 V/2 A or more. The USB-A port on your laptop is perfectly suitable for this.

#### 3. 2 USB-A and 2 HDMI

If your laptop does not have enough HDMI ports, use a compatible HDMI hub or adapter. This setup requires four cables plus an additional hub or adapter.

**Steps:**

1. Connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop using an HDMI cable and a USB-C to USB-A cable.
2. Plug the HDMI hub or adapter into your laptop, then connect the other side of the Screenmate using a second HDMI cable and another USB-C to USB-A cable.

*Image alt:* Two HDMI connections and two USB-A connections to the Screenmate Expand via a hub

> **Note callout:**
> **Note:** the HDMI port does not supply power. Connect the Screenmate to a power source — for example via the USB-A port on your laptop.

## fr/manuals/one-4k/installation.mdx

**Frontmatter title:** "Installation"
**Frontmatter description:** "Connecting your Screenmate One 4K"

### Getting started

- The Screenmate switches on automatically as soon as you connect it with the right cables.
- Always use the supplied cables for optimal performance.
- Your computer automatically recognises the video signal and adapts the display settings.
- If this does not work straight away, manually select the correct input source in your device's display settings.
- If your device supports charging over USB-C, the screen charges automatically as soon as the charger is connected to the Screenmate.
- With Power Delivery (PD) support, the Screenmate switches automatically to fast-charging mode.
- When a signal is received via the USB-C port, the Screenmate also automatically recognises connected USB 2.0 accessories, such as a mouse or a keyboard.

### Connection options

The Screenmate One 4K supports five connection scenarios. Choose the one that matches your device and the cables you have.

#### 1. Laptop via USB-C cable

Connect the Screenmate to your laptop using the USB-C to USB-C cable. A single USB-C cable carries both the video signal and power.

> **Note callout:**
> **Important:** if the connected device does not supply enough power, the monitor must be powered separately by an additional power source.

*Image alt:* USB-C connection to the laptop

Need extra power? Then use the USB-C to USB-A cable with a mains adapter:

*Image alt:* USB-C connection to the laptop with additional power

#### 2. Laptop via HDMI cable

Your laptop has no USB-C port? Connect the Screenmate using an HDMI cable for the video signal, combined with a USB-C to USB-A cable for power.

The USB-A cable can also be plugged into a USB-A port on the laptop for power.

*Image alt:* HDMI connection to the laptop

#### 3. Phone or tablet via USB-C cable

With a single USB-C cable, you can connect your phone or tablet directly to the Screenmate's USB-C port, for both the video signal and power. Note: your phone or tablet must have a USB-C port that supports video — not all USB-C ports can transmit a video signal.

*Image alt:* USB-C connection to the phone or tablet

If the device does not supply enough power, power the monitor separately from an additional power source:

*Image alt:* USB-C connection to the phone or tablet with additional power

#### 4. USB-C games console

To use the Screenmate with a games console that has a USB-C connection, follow these steps:

**Power:** plug the supplied mains adapter into a power socket and connect it to the monitor using the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**Console connection:** use the USB-C to USB-C cable to connect the monitor's USB-C port to the console's USB-C port.

Once both connections are made, the console's video signal appears automatically on the monitor.

*Image alt:* USB-C connection to a games console

#### 5. Devices with an HDMI port

To connect the Screenmate to an HDMI device — such as a PC, a laptop, an Xbox, a PlayStation 4/5 or a Nintendo Charging Dock (and not the Nintendo Switch console itself) — follow these steps:

**Power:** plug the supplied mains adapter into a power socket and connect it to the monitor using the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**HDMI device connection:** use the Mini-HDMI to HDMI cable to connect the monitor's Mini-HDMI port to the device's HDMI port.

Once both connections are made, the HDMI device's video signal appears automatically on the monitor.

*Image alt:* HDMI connection to a PC, a console or a camera
