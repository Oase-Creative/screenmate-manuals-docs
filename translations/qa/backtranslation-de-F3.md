## Safety instructions

Read the following instructions carefully before you use the monitor. These instructions ensure safe operation and extend the lifespan of your Screenmate.

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface before use.
2. Use only the supplied AC/DC power adapter as the power supply.
3. Place the power adapter in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure the socket is properly earthed and suitable for the correct current.
5. The monitor works with a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Use the device only with a 5 V power source via the matching cable.
7. Avoid exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity, in order to prevent damage to the electronics.
8. Clean the screen only with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning agents.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor and protect the screen from impacts, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Keep the ventilation openings clear and ensure sufficient air circulation, in order to prevent overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or transmitter installations, in order to prevent interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between −20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch the screen off when you are not using it.

## Using the OSD menu

1. Press the **M** (Menu) button to open the OSD menu.
2. Use the **+** and **−** buttons to select the setting you want.
3. Press the **M** (Menu) button to confirm your selection.
4. Hold the **M** (Menu) button pressed to go back to the previous page.

**Image alt:** OSD menu Backlight

## Backlight

- **Brightness (BRIGHTNESS):** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Contrast (CONTRAST):** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **ECO mode (ECO):** Preset picture modes for various purposes of use (Standard, Game, Movie, Text, FPS, RTS, Energy Saving).
- **DCR (Dynamic Contrast Ratio):** Choose ON or OFF to switch dynamic contrast on or off.
- **Sharpness (SHARPNESS):** Adjustable from 0 to 4.

## Picture settings

**Image alt:** OSD menu Picture settings

- **Aspect ratio (ASPECT):** Switch the aspect ratio between 4:3 and WIDE.

## Colour settings

**Image alt:** OSD menu Colour settings

- **Colour temperature (COLOR TEMP.):** Set the RGB values manually or choose a preset mode (Warm, Cool, User, Standard).
- **Red (RED):** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Green (GREEN):** Adjustable from 0 to 100.
- **Blue (BLUE):** Adjustable from 0 to 100.

## Settings

**Image alt:** OSD menu Settings

- **Language (LANGUAGE):** Choose the OSD language. Available languages: English, French, German, Simplified Chinese, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Polish, Dutch, Japanese, Korean.
- **OSD timer (OSD TIMER):** Set the time after which the settings menu closes automatically (10–60 seconds).
- **Transparency (TRANSPARENCY):** Adjust the transparency of the settings menu.

## Reset

**Image alt:** OSD menu Reset

- **Reset (RESET):** Choose RESET to reset all settings to the factory settings.
- **HDR mode (HDR MODE):** Activate HDR (High Dynamic Range), if the device is compatible. Available modes: Off, Auto, 2084.

## Other

**Image alt:** OSD menu Other

- **Signal source (SOURCE):** Choose from two signal sources: Type-C1 / Type-C2 and HDMI.
- **Low Blue Light (LOW BLUE LIGHT):** Reduces the amount of blue light from the screen and thereby relieves the eyes.
