alt: "Icon USB-C Power Delivery"

This port only takes in power and is used for:
- Additional power supply when your laptop does not deliver enough power
- Reverse charging: charging your laptop via the Screenmate

## de/manuals/onecable/troubleshooting.mdx

**Frontmatter title:** "FAQ"
**Frontmatter description:** "Frequently asked questions and troubleshooting"

## FAQ

### My screen stays black and shows no image after connecting. What should I do?

Check whether the indicator light of the mainboard is lit. Make sure both ends of the connection cable are properly connected. Check whether your laptop delivers enough power to supply the Screenmate.

### The image is unstable, flickers or drops out occasionally. What can this be caused by?

Check whether the laptop delivers enough power. If necessary, connect an external power supply, for example a USB-C laptop charger, a DC power adapter with 5 V/3 A or a PD charger.

### My laptop has no USB-C port. How can I still use the Screenmate?

Use the double USB-A-to-USB-C cable:

Connect the black USB-A plug to a USB-A port of the laptop (for data). Connect the red USB-A plug to another USB-A port (for power).

Does your laptop have only one USB-A port?

Connect the black USB-A plug to the laptop. Connect the red USB-A plug to a charger or an external power source (e.g. DC 5 V/3 A or a PD charger). Then connect the USB-C end of the cable to the Screenmate.

### A start-up logo appears, but no image is shown on my MacBook. What should I do?

Check whether the driver icon is visible at the top right in your Mac's menu bar. Don't see the icon? Open your Mac's Launchpad, look for the driver and start it manually.

### Can the Screenmate charge my laptop when it is connected to a power source?

That is possible, provided the following conditions are met:

The USB-C port of your laptop supports Power Delivery (PD). The connection is made with a USB-C cable. The power supply is PD-compatible.

Connection method: Connect the USB-C port of your laptop to the Screenmate via a USB-C cable. Connect your laptop charger to the power USB-C port of the Screenmate, in order to enable reverse charging and to improve the screen brightness automatically. Does your laptop charger have no USB-C output? Then use a PD charger of at least 45 W.

## Safety instructions

Always use this product in a safe and responsible manner. Read the following instructions carefully in order to avoid risks such as electric shock or fire.

### Read before use

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface.
2. Use only an AC/DC power adapter as the power supply. Place it in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
3. Make sure the socket is properly earthed and suitable for the correct current.
4. The required DC input lies between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
5. Avoid damp rooms and accumulations of dust, in order to prevent damage to the electronics.
6. Protect the screen from impacts and pressure from outside.
7. Do not use sharp objects on or around the screen.
8. Suitable for private and commercial use.
9. Recommended ambient temperature: between −20 °C and 60 °C.
10. Switch the screen off when you are not using it.
