## Safety instructions

Read the following instructions carefully before you use the monitor. These instructions ensure safe operation and extend the service life of your Screenmate.

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface before use.
2. Use exclusively the supplied AC/DC power adapter as the power source.
3. Place the power adapter in a well-ventilated location, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure that the socket is properly earthed and suitable for the correct current rating.
5. The monitor operates with a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Use the device exclusively with a 5 V power source via the appropriate cable.
7. Avoid exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity, in order to prevent damage to the electronics.
8. Clean the screen only with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning agents.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor and protect the screen from impacts, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Keep the ventilation openings clear and ensure sufficient air circulation, in order to prevent overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or transmitter installations, in order to prevent interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between −20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch the screen off when you are not using it.

**Observations:** items 5 and 6 are in tension — 5 states a 5–20 V DC input range, 6 says to use only a 5 V source. Rendered as written.

## Protective cap

## Installation steps

1. Unpack the Screenmate.
2. Unfold the two screens in the direction shown in figure 2.
3. Place the bracket against the back of your laptop screen and make sure it grips firmly, so that your screen is securely clamped.
4. Lift the stand and pull it out while holding the button pressed. The stand is integrated into the back of the Screenmate and provides additional support. Press the button again to push the stand back in.
5. Connect the right cables: plug one end into your laptop and the other into the Screenmate. Under [Ports and buttons](/de/manuals/expand/controls) you will find an overview of all available ports.

**Image alt:** Installation steps 1 to 6

## Connection options

The Screenmate Expand can be connected in three ways. Choose the variant that matches the ports on your laptop.

### 1. Two USB-C cables

If your laptop has two USB-C ports, connect your laptop to the Screenmate with the two supplied USB-C cables. Each cable carries both video and power.

**Note callout**
**Important:** Make sure that you use the correct USB-C port on your Screenmate.

**Image alt:** Two USB-C cables connecting the laptop to the Screenmate Expand

### 2. 1× USB-C, 1× HDMI and 1× USB-A

If your laptop has a USB-C port, an HDMI port and a USB-A port, you can connect the Screenmate as follows:

Connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop with the supplied USB-C cable. You connect the other side with the HDMI cable and the USB-C-to-USB-A cable.

**Image alt:** USB-C, HDMI and USB-A connection on the Screenmate Expand

**Note callout**
**Note:** The HDMI port does not supply power. The Screenmate must therefore be connected to a power source of 5 V/2 A or more. The USB-A port on your laptop is well suited for this.

### 3. 2× USB-A and 2× HDMI

If your laptop does not have enough HDMI ports, use a suitable HDMI hub or adapter. For this variant you need four cables plus an additional hub or adapter.

**Steps:**

1. Connect one side of the Screenmate to your laptop with an HDMI cable and a USB-C-to-USB-A cable.
2. Plug the HDMI hub or adapter into your laptop and then connect the other side of the Screenmate with a second HDMI cable and another USB-C-to-USB-A cable.

**Image alt:** Two HDMI and two USB-A connections to the Screenmate Expand via a hub

**Note callout**
**Note:** The HDMI port does not supply power. Connect the Screenmate to a power source – for example via the USB-A port on your laptop.

## First steps

- The Screenmate switches on automatically as soon as you connect it with the right cables.
- Always use the supplied cables for optimal performance.
- Your computer detects the video signal automatically and adjusts the display settings.
- If it does not work straight away, select the correct input source manually via your device's display settings.
- If your device supports charging via USB-C, the screen is charged automatically as soon as you connect the charger to the Screenmate.
- Where Power Delivery (PD) is supported, the Screenmate automatically activates the fast-charging function.
- As soon as a signal is received via the USB-C port, the Screenmate additionally detects connected USB 2.0 accessories automatically, such as a mouse or a keyboard.

## Connection options

The Screenmate One 4K can be connected in five ways. Choose the variant that matches your device and the available cables.

### 1. Laptop via USB-C cable

Connect the Screenmate to your laptop with the USB-C-to-USB-C cable. A single USB-C cable carries both the video signal and the power.

**Note callout**
**Important:** If the connected device does not supply enough power, the monitor must be powered separately from an additional power source.

**Image alt:** USB-C connection with the laptop

Do you need more power? In that case use the USB-C-to-USB-A cable together with a power adapter:

**Image alt:** USB-C connection with the laptop and additional power supply

### 2. Laptop via HDMI cable

Your laptop has no USB-C port? Connect the Screenmate via an HDMI cable for the video signal and, in addition, via a USB-C-to-USB-A cable for the power supply.

You can also connect the USB-A cable to a USB-A port on the laptop for the power supply.

**Image alt:** HDMI connection with the laptop

### 3. Smartphone or tablet via USB-C cable

With a single USB-C cable you can connect your smartphone or tablet directly to the USB-C port of the Screenmate – for video signal and power supply at the same time. Note: your smartphone or tablet must have a USB-C port with video support – not every USB-C port can output video.

**Image alt:** USB-C connection with smartphone or tablet

If the device does not supply enough power, power the monitor separately from an additional power source:

**Image alt:** USB-C connection with smartphone or tablet and additional power supply

### 4. Games console via USB-C

To use the Screenmate with a games console that has a USB-C port, proceed as follows:

**Power supply:** Connect the supplied power adapter to a socket and connect it to the monitor via the USB-C-to-USB-A cable.

**Connecting the console:** Using the USB-C-to-USB-C cable, connect the USB-C port of the monitor to the USB-C port of the console.

As soon as both connections are made, the video signal of the console appears automatically on the monitor.

**Image alt:** USB-C connection with a games console

### 5. Devices with an HDMI port

To connect the Screenmate to an HDMI device – such as a PC, a laptop, an Xbox, a PlayStation 4/5 or a Nintendo Charging Dock (not the Nintendo Switch itself) – proceed as follows:

**Power supply:** Connect the supplied power adapter to a socket and connect it to the monitor via the USB-C-to-USB-A cable.

**Connecting the HDMI device:** Using the Mini-HDMI-to-HDMI cable, connect the Mini-HDMI port of the monitor to the HDMI port of the device.

As soon as both connections are made, the video signal of the HDMI device appears automatically on the monitor.

**Image alt:** HDMI connection with a PC, a console or a camera

Connect the Screenmate to your laptop with the USB-C-to-USB-C cable. A single USB-C cable carries both the video signal and the power.

**Note callout**
**Important:** If the connected device does not supply enough power, the monitor must be powered separately from an additional power source.

**Image alt:** Connection to a laptop via USB-C, with and without an additional power source

### 2. Laptop via HDMI cable

Your laptop has no USB-C port? Connect the Screenmate via an HDMI cable for the video signal and, in addition, via a USB-C-to-USB-A cable for the power supply.

You can also connect the USB-A cable to a USB-A port on the laptop for the power supply.

**Image alt:** Connection to a laptop via HDMI and USB-A

### 3. Smartphone or tablet via USB-C cable

With a single USB-C cable you can connect your smartphone or tablet directly to the USB-C port of the Screenmate – for video signal and power supply at the same time.

If the device does not supply enough power, power the monitor separately from an additional power source.

**Image alt:** Connection to a smartphone or tablet via USB-C, with and without an additional power source

### 4. Games console via USB-C

To use the Screenmate with a games console that has a USB-C port, proceed as follows:

**Power supply:** Connect the supplied power adapter to a socket and connect it to the monitor via the USB-C-to-USB-A cable.

**Connecting the console:** Using the USB-C-to-USB-C cable, connect the USB-C port of the monitor to the USB-C port of the console.

As soon as both connections are made, the video signal of the console appears automatically on the monitor.

**Image alt:** Connection to a games console with a USB-C port

### 5. Devices with an HDMI port

To connect the Screenmate to an HDMI device – such as a PC, a laptop, an Xbox, a PlayStation 4/5 or a Nintendo Charging Dock (not the Nintendo Switch itself) – proceed as follows:

**Power supply:** Connect the supplied power adapter to a socket and connect it to the monitor via the USB-C-to-USB-A cable.

**Connecting the HDMI device:** Using the Mini-HDMI-to-HDMI cable, connect the Mini-HDMI port of the monitor to the HDMI port of the device.

As soon as both connections are made, the video signal of the HDMI device appears automatically on the monitor.

**Image alt:** Connection to devices with an HDMI port

## Operation and OSD menu

**Switching on:** Press and hold the power button to switch the monitor on. The monitor also switches on automatically when it is supplied with power.

**Opening the OSD menu:** Press the power button to open the OSD menu.

**Navigating:** Use the + or − button to navigate through the menu. The selected option is highlighted in yellow.

**Selecting:** Press the power button to select an option or to confirm a setting.

**Adjusting settings:** Use the + or − button to change a value, and press the power button to save.

**Back:** Press and hold the − button to return to the previous menu.

### Quick access for volume and brightness

- Press the **+** button to adjust the brightness.
- Press the **−** button to adjust the volume.
