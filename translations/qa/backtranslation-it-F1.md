**Frontmatter description:** "Important safety information and warnings"

## Safety instructions

Read the following guidance carefully before using the monitor. It ensures safe operation and extends the life of your Screenmate.

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface before use.
2. Use only the supplied AC/DC power adapter as the power source.
3. Place the power adapter in a well-ventilated spot, away from heat sources.
4. Make sure the mains socket is earthed and suitable for the correct amperage.
5. The monitor works with a DC input between 5 V and 20 V (with a tolerance of ±2 V).
6. Use the device only with a 5 V power source, via the appropriate cable.
7. Avoid exposure to moisture, dust and static electricity to prevent damage to the electronic components.
8. Clean the screen only with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use liquids or aggressive cleaning agents.
9. Do not touch the device with wet hands and do not use it in damp environments.
10. Do not drop the monitor and protect the screen from impacts, pressure or sharp objects.
11. Do not block the ventilation openings and ensure sufficient air circulation to prevent overheating.
12. Limit exposure to strong magnetic fields or to transmitting equipment to avoid interference.
13. Recommended ambient temperature: between -20 °C and 60 °C.
14. Switch off the screen when it is not in use.

## Installation instructions

1. Carefully take the Screenmate out of the box.
2. Open the two screens as shown in image 2.

## Connection options

Note: make sure you use the correct USB-C port on your Screenmate.

*Image alt:* Connection options

After the driver has been installed, the Screenmate can be used with a single USB-C cable, provided the laptop delivers a power output above 10 W.

Is the power output below 10 W? Then additional power is needed for the Screenmate to work stably.

## Use with 2 USB cables

Note: connect both USB-A cables to your laptop or to a suitable adapter.

Make sure you first connect both cables to the laptop and only then the other end to the Screenmate.

This way you avoid an unstable power supply.

Or

Do you have only one USB port on your laptop?

Then connect the other USB cable to a mains socket.

*Image alt:* Use with 2 cables, part 2

## Charging the Screenmate OneCable

When the Screenmate is connected to an external power source, for example your laptop's USB-C power adapter, and the laptop is connected via a USB-C port with Power Delivery, reverse charging mode is activated automatically.

You can often recognise this port by a charging symbol or a lightning bolt.

1. Connect your laptop to the Screenmate with a USB-C cable (with full functionality)
2. Connect the USB-C power adapter to the Power port of the Screenmate

Your laptop is now charging via the Screenmate and the screen brightness is optimised automatically.

Note: use a power adapter of at least 45 W. Don't have a USB-C charger? Use a suitable power adapter.

## it/manuals/onecable/troubleshooting.mdx

**Frontmatter title:** "FAQ"
**Frontmatter description:** "Frequently asked questions and troubleshooting"

## FAQ

### The screen stays black and no image appears after connecting. What can I do?

Check whether the motherboard indicator light is on. Make sure both ends of the connection cable are properly connected. Check whether your laptop delivers enough power to support the Screenmate.

### The screen is unstable, flickers or cuts out from time to time. What could be the cause?

Check whether the laptop delivers enough power. If necessary, connect an external power source, such as a USB-C laptop charger, a 5 V/3 A DC power adapter or a PD charger.

### My laptop does not have a USB-C port. Can I still use the Screenmate?

Use the double USB-A to USB-C cable:

Connect the black USB-A plug to a USB-A port on the laptop (for data). Connect the red USB-A plug to another USB-A port (for power).

Does your laptop have only one USB-A port?

Connect the black USB-A plug to the laptop. Connect the red USB-A plug to a charger or an external power source (for example DC 5 V/3 A or a PD charger). Then connect the USB-C end of the cable to the Screenmate.

### The boot logo appears, but there is no image on the MacBook. What can I do?

Check whether the driver icon is visible in the menu bar at the top right on the Mac. Don't see the icon? Go to the Mac's Launchpad, look for the driver and start it manually.

### Can the Screenmate charge my laptop when it is connected to a power source?

It is possible, provided the following conditions are met:

Your laptop's USB-C port supports Power Delivery (PD). The connection is made with a USB-C cable. The power supply is PD-compatible.

Connection method: connect your laptop's USB-C port to the Screenmate with a USB-C cable. Connect the laptop's charger to the Screenmate's USB-C Power port to activate reverse charging and automatically improve the screen brightness. Does your laptop's charger not have a USB-C output? Then use a PD charger of at least 45 W.

## Connection options

The Infinity Lite supports two main connection modes, depending on your laptop's ports.

**USB-C connection**

**USB and HDMI connection**

If your laptop has a USB-A or USB-C port **and** an HDMI port, use two cables:

- A USB-A or USB-C to USB-C cable (for the power)

> **Warning callout:**
