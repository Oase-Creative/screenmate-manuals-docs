# Screenmate Localization — Consolidated Flag Report

This document merges every open question raised during the DE / FR / IT localization of the Screenmate manuals into one client-facing review list. It was assembled from three source families: (a) all **35 per-product flag files** in `translations/flags/` (12 DE, 11 FR, 12 IT — one per product slug plus a `*-shared.md` for the safety and display-settings chapters), which record EN↔NL disagreements found while translating; (b) the **"EN-source issues" sections** of the three back-translation adjudications `translations/qa/backtranslation-review-de.md` (27 items), `-fr.md` (22 items) and `-it.md` (23 items), which list defects the reviewers found in the **English source itself**; and (c) the **skip lists** of `translations/qa/fixes-de.md`, `fixes-fr.md` and `fixes-it.md`, which record items deliberately *not* auto-fixed. The three language teams worked blind of each other, so the same underlying defect was frequently caught two or three times independently — those have been merged into single rows, with every originating source named in the "Found By" column. Nothing here is invented: every row cites a real file and line. §1 collects defects to fix in the English source; §2 collects observations about the Dutch pages (NL is frozen — nothing was edited there); §3 collects questions only the client can answer because they concern the physical product; §4 collects internal items parked for a later pass.
