# Screenmate Localization — Consolidated Flag Report

This document merges every open question raised during the DE / FR / IT localization of the Screenmate manuals into one client-facing review list. It was assembled from three source families: (a) all **35 per-product flag files** in `translations/flags/` (12 DE, 11 FR, 12 IT — one per product slug plus a `*-shared.md` for the safety and display-settings chapters), which record EN↔NL disagreements found while translating; (b) the **"EN-source issues" sections** of the three back-translation adjudications `translations/qa/backtranslation-review-de.md` (27 items), `-fr.md` (22 items) and `-it.md` (23 items), which list defects the reviewers found in the **English source itself**; (c) the **skip lists** of `translations/qa/fixes-de.md`, `fixes-fr.md` and `fixes-it.md`, which record items deliberately *not* auto-fixed; and (d) the three **round-4 source-flag files** `translations/qa/round4/source-flags-de.md`, `-fr.md` and `-it.md`, which quarantine every round-4 finding that turned out to be a faithful rendering of a defective source rather than a translation error. The three language teams worked blind of each other, so the same underlying defect was frequently caught two or three times independently — those have been merged into single rows, with every originating source named in the "Found By" column. Nothing here is invented: every row cites a real file and line. §1 collects defects to fix in the English source; §2 collects observations about the Dutch pages (NL is frozen — nothing was edited there); §3 collects questions only the client can answer because they concern the physical product; §4 collects internal items parked for a later pass.

**Version 2 — 12 August 2026, updated after the round-4 native-reader review.** Six independent native-reader reviews (two per language, each reading the pages as a customer would, with no access to the source) and three line-by-line safety alignment audits produced roughly 122 further findings that traced back to the English or Dutch source rather than to the translations. Those have been merged in here. Where a round-4 finding corroborates an item that was already listed, the existing row now carries the additional evidence; where it is genuinely new, the row — or the sentence inside an existing row — is tagged **[R4]**. None of these were "fixed" in German, French or Italian: doing so would have made the translated pages disagree with the English on a point of fact, so they were quarantined for you instead. Current totals: **65 English-source defects (§1), 22 Dutch-side observations (§2), 15 product-fact questions (§3), 11 deferred minors (§4)**. The 5 V vs 5–20 V safety contradiction has been moved to the top of §1 as the highest-priority item in this report.
