## What is the Screenmate Expand?

The Screenmate Expand is a triple-screen portable monitor that clips onto your laptop and unfolds two Full HD displays — one on each side — turning your laptop into a three-screen workstation. It is available in two sizes: **14"** and **15.6"**. The specifications for each size are listed below.

## Package Contents

- **Screenmate Expand**
- **2x USB-C to USB-C cables**
- **2x Mini-HDMI to HDMI cables**
- **2x USB-C to USB-A cables**
- **Protective case**
- **Cable organizer**
- **6x protective clips**

<Note>
**Important Information:**

Take good care of your Screenmate Expand and do not press on the screens to prevent damage. Do not rotate the screens beyond the maximum angles indicated below.

- **Left screen:** 0° – 235°
- **Right screen:** 0° – 180°

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2014%20-%20Handleiding%20images/rotation-angles.png" alt="Maximum rotation angles for left and right screens" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

The Screenmate consumes a small amount of power in standby mode. For energy-efficient operation, we recommend disconnecting the power cable when the monitor is not in use.
</Note>
