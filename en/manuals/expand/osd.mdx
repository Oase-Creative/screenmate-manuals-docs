## Using the OSD

1. Press the **M (Menu)** button to open the OSD menu.
2. Use the **+ / −** buttons to select your desired setting.
3. Press **M (Menu)** to confirm your choice.
4. Press and hold **M (Menu)** to return to the previous page.

## OSD Settings

### 1. Backlight

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-backlight.png" alt="OSD Backlight menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Brightness (BRIGHTNESS):** Adjust the screen brightness (0–100).
- **Contrast (CONTRAST):** Adjust screen contrast (0–100).
- **ECO mode (ECO):** Preset display modes (Standard, Game, Movie, Text, FPS, RTS, Energy Saving).
- **DCR (Dynamic Contrast Ratio):** Turn dynamic contrast ON or OFF.
- **Sharpness (SHARPNESS):** Adjust image sharpness (0–4).

### 2. Image

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-image.png" alt="OSD Image menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Aspect (ASPECT):** Switch between **4:3** and **WIDE**.

### 3. Color

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-color.png" alt="OSD Color menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Color Temp. (COLOR TEMP.):** Set RGB values manually or pick a preset (Warm, Cool, User, Standard).
- **Red (RED):** Adjust the red channel (0–100).
- **Green (GREEN):** Adjust the green channel (0–100).
- **Blue (BLUE):** Adjust the blue channel (0–100).

### 4. OSD Settings

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-set.png" alt="OSD Settings menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Language (LANGUAGE):** Choose the OSD language. Available languages: English, French, German, Simplified Chinese, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Polish, Dutch, Japanese, Korean.
- **OSD Timer (OSD TIMER):** Set how long the settings menu stays on screen before closing automatically (10–60 seconds).
- **Transparency (TRANSPARENCY):** Adjust the transparency of the settings menu.

### 5. Reset

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-reset.png" alt="OSD Reset menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Reset (RESET):** Choose RESET to restore all settings to factory defaults.
- **HDR Mode (HDR MODE):** Enable HDR (High Dynamic Range) if your device supports it. Available modes: Off, Auto, 2084.

### 6. Other

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Expand%2015.6%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-other.png" alt="OSD Other menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Source (SOURCE):** Choose between two signal sources: Type-C1 / Type-C2 and HDMI.
- **Low Blue Light (LOW BLUE LIGHT):** Reduces the amount of blue light from the screen to ease eye strain.
