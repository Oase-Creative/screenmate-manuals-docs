## Ports and Buttons

This section provides an overview of the physical ports and control buttons on the Screenmate Infinity Lite.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/ports-buttons.png" alt="Screenmate Infinity Lite ports and buttons overview" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### USB-C (for HDMI > USB-C)

The leftmost bottom port. Reserved for the HDMI to USB-C cable when using a separate video and power setup.

### USB-C

The second bottom USB-C port. Carries both video and power over a single USB-C to USB-C cable, or is used for power when the HDMI port carries the video signal.

### 3.5 mm Headphone Jack

For connecting headphones or external speakers.

### Speaker

Built-in speaker at the top edge of the unit.

### LED Indicator

Status LED on the back of the unit.

## Buttons and Functions

The Infinity Lite has three buttons on the bottom edge.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20Lite%20-%20Handleiding%20images/buttons-diagram.png" alt="Infinity Lite buttons diagram" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### Menu / Selection / Confirm Button

- Press to open the on-screen menu or to confirm a selection.
- Press and hold for **2 seconds** to go back.
- Press and hold for **3 seconds** to turn the screen off.

### Left "Min" Button

Toggle left – adjusts the backlight (brightness).

### Right "Plus" Button

Toggle right – adjusts the volume.
