## Safety Instructions

Always use this product in a safe and responsible manner. Read the following guidelines carefully to avoid risks such as electric shock or fire.

### Read this before use

1. Remove the protective film from the screen surface.
2. Only use an AC/DC adapter as power supply. Place it in a well-ventilated area, away from heat sources.
3. Make sure the power outlet is properly grounded and suitable for the correct amperage.
4. The required DC input is between 5V and 20V (with a tolerance of ±2V).
5. Avoid humid spaces and dust accumulation to prevent damage to the electronics.
6. Protect the screen against impacts and external pressure.
7. Do not use sharp objects on or around the screen.
8. Suitable for both home and business use.
9. Recommended ambient temperature: between -20°C and 60°C.
10. Turn the screen off when you are not using it.
