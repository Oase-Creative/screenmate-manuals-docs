## What is the Screenmate One 4K OLED?

The Screenmate One 4K OLED is a 15.6" 4K UHD portable monitor with an AM-OLED panel and a built-in stand. The OLED panel delivers deep blacks, vivid colours, and a 100,000:1 contrast ratio — ideal for video, photo and creative work. It connects via USB-C or HDMI and supports both video and power over a single USB-C cable on compatible devices.
