## Safety Instructions

Always use this product in a safe and responsible way. Read the following guidelines carefully to avoid risks such as electric shock or fire.

### Check before use

- Remove the protective film from the screen surface.
- Use only an AC/DC adapter as power supply. Place it in a well-ventilated area, away from heat sources.
- Make sure the power outlet is properly grounded and suitable for the correct amperage.
- The required DC input is between 5V and 20V (with a tolerance of ±2V).
- Avoid humid spaces and dust build-up to prevent damage to the electronics.
- Protect the screen against impact and external pressure.
- Do not use sharp objects on or around the screen.
- Suitable for both home and business use.
- Recommended ambient temperature: between -20°C and 60°C.
- Turn the screen off when not in use.
