The Screenmate can be connected in the following ways:

- 2× USB-C
- 1× USB-C & 1× USB & 1× HDMI

## Connection Options

### 1. Dual USB-C connection

Does your laptop have two USB-C ports that support both video and power? Then you can connect the Screenmate directly with two USB-C cables. Plug each USB-C cable into the port marked with the **blue sticker** on the back of the screen.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-1-dual-usbc.png" alt="Dual USB-C connection" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 2. USB-C + HDMI & USB-A connection

Does your laptop have one USB-C port that supports video and power, plus a USB-A and HDMI port? Follow these steps:

1. Connect the first screen with the USB-C cable. Plug it into the port marked with the **blue sticker** on the back of the screen.
2. Connect the second screen with the HDMI to USB-C cable. Plug it into the port marked with the **red sticker** on the back of the screen.

<Note>
**Important:** If one of the screens shows no signal or the signal is unstable, connect an external power supply with a USB-A to USB-C cable.
</Note>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-2-usbc-hdmi.png" alt="USB-C with HDMI and USB-A connection" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### Smartphones and game consoles

The Screenmate also works with a smartphone, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation, or Xbox.

- Use a USB-C hub or a switch dock (such as the one for the Nintendo Switch) and connect an external power supply.
- Connecting a smartphone directly via USB-C? Make sure the device supports video output over USB-C and connect an external power supply here as well.

<Note>
**Important:** Not every USB-C port supports video output. Check this in your device's specifications first.
</Note>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-3-devices.png" alt="Smartphone and console connection" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

## Setup

### Unfolding the screen

1. Detach the screen from the stand.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-1-release.png" alt="Detach the screen from the stand" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

2. Open the stand. The stand opens to a maximum angle of 235°.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-2-open-stand.png" alt="Open the stand to 235 degrees" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

3. Gently slide the frame apart.
4. Attach the screens to one another following the labels: **"L"** for **Left** and **"R"** for **Right**.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-3-4-frame-LR.png" alt="Slide frame apart and attach L and R screens" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

5. Place the stand behind your laptop screen. Make sure the center piece sits neatly in the middle of your laptop. Each screen rotates a maximum of 90°.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/install-5-position.png" alt="Position the stand behind your laptop" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### Setup instructions

Each screen has a maximum rotation of 90°.

<Note>
**Important:** While rotating, the screen and the stand must remain parallel to prevent scratches.
</Note>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/setup-rotation.png" alt="Maximum 90 degree rotation" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### Dual screen — front and back horizontal

1. Open the movable arms of the left and right stand.
2. Attach the right screen to the movable arm of the left stand (for horizontal viewing).
3. Place the left screen on the movable arm of the right stand to complete the setup.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/dual-horizontal.png" alt="Dual screen horizontal setup" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### Dual screen — front and back vertical

1. Open the movable arms of the left and right stand.
2. Attach the right screen to the movable arm of the left stand (for vertical viewing).
3. Place the left screen on the movable arm of the right stand to complete the setup.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/dual-vertical.png" alt="Dual screen vertical setup" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

<Note>
**Tip:** Use the included stability rubbers if your monitor doesn't sit completely steady.
</Note>

## Storage

### Folding the screen back up

1. Remove the left and right screens from the stand.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/storage-1-remove.png" alt="Remove the screens from the stand" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

2. Fold the stand closed.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/storage-2-fold.png" alt="Fold the stand closed" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

3. Place the screens back into the stand for compact storage.

   - The silicone strip on the right screen should line up exactly with the edge of the left screen.
   - The protruding part of the screen must drop neatly into the groove on the stand for a snug fit.

4. Place the fully folded set into the included leather carry pouch.

   <img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Infinity%20-%20Handleiding%20images/storage-final.png" alt="Reseat the screens and place into the carry pouch" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />
