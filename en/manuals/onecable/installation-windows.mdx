### Windows 10 or higher

The required drivers are automatically installed as soon as you connect the Screenmate via a USB-C or USB-A cable.

Make sure your laptop is connected to the internet. After successful installation, it is recommended to restart your laptop before using the Screenmate.

### Manual Installation (if needed)

Is the driver not automatically installed?

Follow these steps:

1. Open **This PC** ('Deze pc') and open the included **DRIVERS (D:)** drive (USB stick).
2. Open the folder that matches your Windows version — for Windows 10 or 11, open the **Win10&11** folder ('mac OS' and 'Win 7&8' folders are also available).
3. Double-click the installer (**RacerDisplayDriver-2024.9.13-en**) and follow the on-screen instructions.
4. Restart your laptop after installation.
5. Connect the Screenmate, it's now ready for use.
