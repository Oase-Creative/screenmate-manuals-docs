## Ports and Buttons

This section provides an overview of all physical ports and control buttons on the Screenmate Panorama.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Panorama%20-%20Handleiding%20images/ports-buttons.png" alt="Overview of the ports and buttons on the Screenmate Panorama" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### USB-C Port

Connect your laptop using a USB-C cable.

### USB-C Charging Port

Connect the supplied 65 W power adapter here using the USB-C to USB-C cable.

### 3 × Mini HDMI Ports

Use a Mini HDMI to HDMI cable to connect your laptop to an individual screen.

### Power Button

- **Long press (1 second):** Switch the monitor off.
- **Short press:** Opens the OSD menu (On-Screen Display) for the currently selected screen.

### Down Button (−)

- Navigate within the menu.
- Decrease values such as brightness or volume.
- Opens the brightness shortcut menu.

### Up Button (+)

- Navigate within the menu.
- Increase values such as brightness or volume.
- Opens the volume shortcut menu.

### Confirm / Exit Button

- **Short press:** Confirms a selection or advances to the next step in the menu.
- **Long press:** Exits the current menu.
