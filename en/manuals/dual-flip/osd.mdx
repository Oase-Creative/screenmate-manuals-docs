## Introduction to the OSD

1. Press the **M** (Menu) button to open the OSD menu.
2. Use the **+ / −** buttons to select your desired settings.
3. Press the **M** (Menu) button to confirm your choice.
4. Press and hold the **M** (Menu) button to return to the previous page.

### 1. Backlight

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-backlight.png" alt="OSD Backlight menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **BRIGHTNESS (0–100):** Adjust the brightness of the screen.
- **CONTRAST (0–100):** Adjust the contrast between light and dark areas.
- **ECO:** Preset display modes for different use cases (Standard, Game, Movie, Text, FPS, RTS, Energy Saving).
- **DCR (Dynamic Contrast Ratio):** Choose ON or OFF to enable or disable dynamic contrast.
- **SHARPNESS (0–4):** Adjust the sharpness of the image.

### 2. Image

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-image.png" alt="OSD Image menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **ASPECT:** Switch the aspect ratio between **4:3** and **WIDE**.

### 3. Color Settings

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-color.png" alt="OSD Color menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **COLOR TEMP:** Manually set the RGB values, or choose a preset (Warm, Cool, User, Standard).
- **RED (0–100):** Adjust the red channel.
- **GREEN (0–100):** Adjust the green channel.
- **BLUE (0–100):** Adjust the blue channel.

### 4. Settings

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-settings.png" alt="OSD Settings menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **LANGUAGE:** Choose the OSD language. Available languages: English, French, German, Simplified Chinese, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Polish, Dutch, Japanese, Korean.
- **OSD TIMER (10–60 seconds):** Set how long the menu stays visible before it closes automatically.
- **TRANSPARENCY:** Adjust the transparency of the menu.

### 5. Reset

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-reset.png" alt="OSD Reset menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **RESET:** Choose RESET to restore all settings to factory defaults.
- **HDR:** Enable HDR (High Dynamic Range) if the connected device supports it. Available modes: Off, Auto, 2084.

### 6. Other

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Dual%20Flip%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-other.png" alt="OSD Other menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **SOURCE:** Choose between three signal sources: Type-C1, Type-C2, and HDMI.
- **LOW BLUE LIGHT:** Reduces the amount of blue light on the screen to reduce eye strain.
