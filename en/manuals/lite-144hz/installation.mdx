## Connection Options

The Screenmate Lite 144 Hz supports five connection scenarios. Pick the one that matches your device and available cables.

### 1. Laptop via USB-C cable

Connect the Screenmate to your laptop with the USB-C to USB-C cable. A single USB-C cable carries both the video signal and power.

<Note>
**Important:** If the connected device doesn't supply enough power, the monitor needs to be powered separately via an additional power source.
</Note>

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-1-usb-c-laptop.png" alt="USB-C connection to laptop" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

Need extra power? Then use the USB-C to USB-A cable in combination with a power adapter:

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-1-usb-c-laptop-power.png" alt="USB-C connection to laptop with extra power" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 2. Laptop via HDMI cable

No USB-C port on your laptop? Connect the Screenmate via an HDMI cable for the video signal together with a USB-C to USB-A cable for power.

The USB-A cable can also be connected to a laptop USB-A port for power.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-2-hdmi-laptop.png" alt="HDMI connection to laptop" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 3. Phone or tablet via USB-C cable

With a single USB-C cable you can connect your phone or tablet directly to the Screenmate's USB-C port for both video and power. Note: your phone or tablet must have a USB-C port with video support — not every USB-C port can output video.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-3-phone-tablet.png" alt="USB-C connection to phone or tablet" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

If the device doesn't deliver enough power, supply the monitor separately via an additional power source:

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-3-phone-tablet-power.png" alt="USB-C connection to phone or tablet with extra power" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 4. USB-C game console

To use the Screenmate with a game console that has a USB-C connection, follow these steps:

**Power supply:** Connect the included power adapter to a wall outlet and connect it to the monitor with the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**Connecting the console:** Use the USB-C to USB-C cable to connect the monitor's USB-C port to the console's USB-C port.

Once both connections are made, the console's video signal appears automatically on the monitor.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/connection-4-game-console-usbc.png" alt="USB-C connection to a game console" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### 5. Devices with an HDMI port

To connect the Screenmate to an HDMI device — such as a PC, laptop, Xbox, PlayStation 4/5, or a Nintendo Charging Dock (not the Nintendo Switch console itself) — follow these steps:

**Power supply:** Connect the included power adapter to a wall outlet and connect it to the monitor with the USB-C to USB-A cable.

**Connecting the HDMI device:** Use the Mini HDMI to HDMI cable to connect the monitor's Mini HDMI port to the device's HDMI port.

Once both connections are made, the HDMI device's video signal appears automatically on the monitor.
