## On-Screen Menu Settings

Press the Power & Return button to open the on-screen menu. Use the scroll wheel to navigate, and press the button again to confirm a selection.

### 1. Brightness

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-brightness.png" alt="OSD Brightness menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Brightness (0–100):** Adjust the brightness of the screen.
- **Contrast (0–100):** Change the contrast to optimize the difference between light and dark areas.
- **Black Level (0–100):** Set the black level for deeper blacks and more detail in dark scenes.
- **Sharpness (0–4):** Increase sharpness to improve image clarity and detail.

### 2. Image Modes

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-image.png" alt="OSD Image menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **ECO modes:** Choose a picture mode based on how you're using the monitor:
  - **Standard:** Default picture mode.
  - **RTS:** Optimized for Real-Time Strategy (RTS) games.
  - **FPS:** Optimized for First-Person Shooter (FPS) games.
  - **Text:** Optimal readability for text.
  - **Movie:** Suited for watching films.
  - **Game:** Optimized for gaming.
- **DCR (Dynamic Contrast Ratio) (ON/OFF):** Automatically adjusts brightness and contrast for better picture quality.
- **Aspect Ratio (16:9 / 4:3):** Switch the aspect ratio between widescreen (16:9) and 4:3 depending on the content.

### 3. Color Settings

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-color.png" alt="OSD Color Temperature menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Color Temperature:** Choose User, Warm, or Cool to adjust the overall color intensity.
- **Red (0–100):** Adjust the brightness of the red RGB value.
- **Green (0–100):** Adjust the brightness of the green RGB value.
- **Blue (0–100):** Adjust the brightness of the blue RGB value.

### 4. OSD Settings

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/osd-settings.png" alt="OSD Settings menu" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

- **Language:** Choose from 12 available languages for the OSD menu display.
- **OSD Timer (10–60 seconds):** Set how long the OSD menu stays visible on the screen.
- **Transparency (0–100):** Adjust the transparency of the OSD menu for a better view.
