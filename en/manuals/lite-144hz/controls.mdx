## Ports and Buttons

This section provides an overview of all physical ports and control buttons on the Screenmate Lite 144 Hz.

<img src="/images/Screenmate%20-%20Lite%20144Hz%20-%20Handleiding%20images/ports-buttons-diagram.png" alt="Screenmate Lite 144 Hz ports and buttons overview" className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200" />

### USB-C Port

For power supply via a USB-C to USB-A cable, or for power **and** video over a USB-C to USB-C cable.

### 3.5mm Headphone Jack

For connecting headphones or external speakers.

### Mini HDMI Port

Easily connect an external device via a Mini HDMI to HDMI cable.

### Scroll Wheel

**General mode**
- Short press: turn the device on
- Long press: turn the device off

**OSD menu mode**
- Short press: go back one step
- Rotate: navigate through menus and adjust settings

### Power & Return Button

**General mode**
- Press to open the on-screen settings menu (OSD)

**OSD menu mode**
- Press to confirm a selection or move to the next option
