## What is the Screenmate Lite?

The Screenmate Lite is a lightweight 15.6" Full HD portable monitor designed to boost your productivity by adding an extra screen to your laptop, phone, tablet, or game console. It connects via USB-C or HDMI and weighs just 609 grams.
